Published stories tagged formula-1.

  • News November 21, 2026

    "Nobody wanted to be at the front": the tow game that wrecked Ferrari's Las Vegas qualifying

    Slipstream tactics decided Las Vegas qualifying. George Russell says getting the tow right was "very high-risk", while Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton both went out in Q2 and will start 11th and 12th before penalties.

  • News November 21, 2026

    Antonelli was fastest in Las Vegas, but a five-place penalty hands Max Verstappen his first pole on the Strip

    Kimi Antonelli topped qualifying for the Las Vegas Grand Prix with a 1:33.893, but a five-place grid penalty for power unit changes drops him to sixth. Max Verstappen starts from pole alongside George Russell, with Lando Norris and Isack Hadjar on row two.

  • News November 20, 2026

    F1’s V8 comeback is “agreed in principle” for 2030, and the numbers say about 1,050bhp in overtake mode

    McLaren CEO Zak Brown says there is an agreement in principle for 2.1-litre V8 turbo hybrid engines from 2030. BBC Sport reports about 780bhp from the combustion engine plus a 200kW electric part, with the rules due at the World Motor Sport Council next month.

  • News November 20, 2026

    F1 is finally getting full-time stewards, and the FIA wants up to 15 of them

    The FIA will start bringing full-time stewards into Formula 1 during the 2027 season, building toward a department of 12 to 15. Part-time and driver stewards will stay in a mixed roster for now.

  • News November 20, 2026

    Russell snatched P1 on the final lap of a soaking Las Vegas FP2, and Verstappen ended up 13th

    George Russell topped a wet second practice in Las Vegas with a 1:47.272 set as the chequered flag fell, 0.071s ahead of Pierre Gasly and 0.333s ahead of Charles Leclerc. A red flag for plastic debris interrupted the session.

  • News November 19, 2026

    "I don't even want to know my heart rate": Verstappen says no to F1's WHOOP TV plan, Leclerc says yes

    Max Verstappen says he will not take part in F1's plan to show drivers' heart rates on TV from 2027, calling it a privacy matter. Charles Leclerc is happy to share; George Russell has doubts about how fans will read the numbers.

  • News November 19, 2026

    Every F1 driver is getting a LEGO minifigure for the first time, and you can pre-order them now

    F1 and the LEGO Group have revealed a Speed Champions range with all 22 drivers as minifigures and all 11 teams' 2026 cars. Sets go on sale on 1 March 2027, with pre-orders open today in select regions.

  • News November 19, 2026

    Drivers' heart rates could be on your TV as early as 2027: F1 announces WHOOP partnership

    F1 has signed a partnership deal with wearables firm WHOOP. Drivers who sign up will wear its bands in the car, and data such as heart rate could feed TV graphics as early as 2027. F1 has not said how many drivers are in.

  • Rules Explainers November 19, 2026

    Hamilton called it "the silliest thing". Now the FIA may change F1's clipping rules for 2027

    The FIA will consult teams on how electrical power is cut back at high speed, the effect drivers call clipping, after Lewis Hamilton said it stops cars completing passes. Nothing has been agreed and the 2026 rules stay as they are.

  • News November 18, 2026

    Piastri set for a 10-place grid penalty at the Las Vegas GP after a fifth combustion engine

    A reliability issue forces McLaren to fit a new internal combustion engine, taking Oscar Piastri past his allowance of four. Both McLarens also get Mercedes' ADUO turbo upgrade, which carries no penalty.

  • Rules Explainers November 18, 2026

    FIA confirms three Straight Mode zones for the Las Vegas GP, including the 1.9km Strip run

    The longest zone runs from after Turn 12 through the Turn 13 kink to Turn 14. Overtake Mode detection is just after Turn 16, giving a chasing car within one second an extra 0.5MJ.

  • Teams November 17, 2026

    Williams will run a one-off chrome livery with Atlassian at the Las Vegas GP

    Alex Albon and Carlos Sainz will also wear matching race suits and gloves. James Vowles says the design reflects the team's near 50-year history, while Atlassian's chief executive talks up AI workflows built for the team.

  • News November 17, 2026

    Norris says a wet Las Vegas GP would be "scary" on Pirelli's current rain tyres

    After the first fully wet weekend of the new rules in Sao Paulo, Lando Norris, Max Verstappen and Carlos Sainz say the intermediates lose grip when cold. Forecasts give a better than even chance of rain in Thursday's FP1.

  • Driver Market November 16, 2026

    Haas keep Rafael Camara for the Las Vegas GP, with Ryo Hirakawa in Bearman's car for FP1

    The 21-year-old Brazilian gets a second F1 weekend after his wet debut in Sao Paulo. Haas have not said who partners Oliver Bearman in Qatar and Abu Dhabi, where Camara is also chasing the F2 title.

  • Teams November 16, 2026

    Wolff says Mercedes could throw their weight behind Russell in the fight for second place

    With both titles won, Toto Wolff says Mercedes want George Russell to finish runner-up to Kimi Antonelli. Russell heads to Las Vegas third, 17 points behind Max Verstappen and one ahead of Lewis Hamilton.

  • Teams November 15, 2026

    Allison: cold tyres, not a broken car, behind Mercedes' strange handling in Brazil

    Mercedes technical director James Allison says puddles stripped the heat from fresh slicks at Interlagos and that both drivers later overworked their rear intermediates, backing deputy team principal Bradley Lord's explanation.

  • Teams November 15, 2026

    Vasseur says Ferrari knew from the first straight that the 2026 title would be hard to win

    The Ferrari team principal says Mercedes were six to seven tenths faster in the opening six races and that straight-line speed held the SF-26 back, but believes Ferrari has matched the pace since Malaysia.

  • News November 15, 2026

    Imola's mayor vows to keep pushing for an F1 return after the circuit is stood down

    Marco Panieri says the city and the Emilia-Romagna region will keep working to bring Formula 1 back, days after F1 confirmed it no longer needs Imola as a back-up for the season finale.

  • Results November 14, 2026

    Audi's appeal over Tsunoda's Monza point to be heard by the FIA on 25 November

    The FIA International Court of Appeal will hear Audi's case in Paris. If it succeeds, Gabriel Bortoleto could move into 10th place and take the final point from the Italian Grand Prix.

  • Rules Explainers November 14, 2026

    Williams' 'ADUO for teams' cost cap plan goes to Monday's F1 Commission

    James Vowles wants a sliding scale that lets teams outside the top four spend more under the cost cap. Only four other teams back it so far, so 2028 looks more realistic than 2027.

  • Driver Market November 13, 2026

    Nikola Tsolov named reserve driver for Red Bull and Racing Bulls in 2027

    Red Bull has made the 19-year-old Bulgarian reserve for both of its Formula 1 teams next year, with simulator work, practice outings and older-car testing, after Racing Bulls kept Lawson and Lindblad.

  • Driver Market November 12, 2026

    Racing Bulls keep Liam Lawson and Arvid Lindblad for 2027

    Racing Bulls said on 12 November that Liam Lawson and Arvid Lindblad will stay as its drivers in 2027, ending talk that Red Bull junior Nikola Tsolov could take Lawson's seat.

  • News November 12, 2026

    George Russell launches a karting academy for 8- and 9-year-olds at Silverstone

    The Mercedes driver's GR63 Karting Academy opens with £25 qualifying sessions at Kart Silverstone in December and ends with 25 young drivers in a fully funded 2027 championship.

  • News November 12, 2026

    Norris questions Leclerc's five-second Sao Paulo penalty for contact with Lawson

    Race winner Lando Norris said on a Twitch stream that Charles Leclerc's late contact with Liam Lawson at Interlagos was 'just racing', disagreeing with the stewards who dropped Leclerc to eighth.

  • News November 11, 2026

    Bearman calls Haas's last three races a 'horrible three weeks' after finishing 17th in Sao Paulo

    In an 11 November Formula1.com interview, Ollie Bearman said he just wanted to go home and reset after a Sao Paulo finish of 17th and two earlier retirements for Haas.

  • News November 11, 2026

    F1 tells Imola it is not needed, and plans to finish 2026 in Qatar and Abu Dhabi

    Formula 1 informed Imola on 10 November that the circuit will not be required as a season-ending standby, Sky Sports reported, confirming the planned Qatar and Abu Dhabi finales unless the security situation changes.

  • News November 11, 2026

    Apple TV to stream the full Las Vegas Grand Prix weekend free in the United States

    Grandprix.com reported on 10 November that U.S. viewers can watch practice, qualifying and the 21 November night race on Apple TV without a subscription, under Apple's first season of U.S. Formula 1 rights.
