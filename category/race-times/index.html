    Mexico City Grand Prix 2026 start times in ET, CT, MT and PT

    The Mexico City Grand Prix starts Sunday, November 1 at 3 PM Eastern, 2 PM Central, 1 PM Mountain and noon Pacific. Daylight saving time ends that morning, so race day runs on standard time.

  • Race Times October 27, 2026

    São Paulo Grand Prix 2026 start times in Eastern, Central, Mountain and Pacific time

    The São Paulo Grand Prix starts Sunday, November 8 at noon Eastern, 11 AM Central, 10 AM Mountain and 9 AM Pacific. US clocks will already be on standard time, and there is no Sprint at Interlagos this year.
