Racing Bulls keep Liam Lawson and Arvid Lindblad for 2027

By Grid and Pit

Racing Bulls said on 12 November that Liam Lawson and Arvid Lindblad will stay as its drivers in 2027, ending talk that Red Bull junior Nikola Tsolov could take Lawson's seat.

Last updated November 12, 2026

Racing Bulls will run an unchanged driver line-up in 2027, with Liam Lawson and Arvid Lindblad both extending their contracts, Formula1.com reported on Thursday morning. The team announced the news in a post on X at about 6 a.m. ET.

The decision settles one of the last open questions on next year's grid. Red Bull had been weighing whether to promote Bulgarian Formula 2 driver Nikola Tsolov in place of Lawson, BBC Sport reported, but chose to keep the current pairing for a second season.

Why Lawson kept his seat

Lawson, 24, has 89 points and sits ninth in the drivers' standings, the best of the midfield, according to Formula1.com's analysis. He has scored in 15 of the 20 Grands Prix this year, two of them while filling in at Red Bull, and qualified third in Sao Paulo before finishing seventh. That was his fourth seventh-place finish in five races, per Formula1.com.

The BBC noted that Lawson has outqualified Lindblad 13 times to nine and scored more than twice as many points. He also stood in at Red Bull for the Dutch, Italian and Spanish Grands Prix while Isack Hadjar recovered from a broken wrist, the BBC said.

Lindblad's rookie year

Lindblad, 19, has 38 points and is 11th in the standings, Formula1.com said. He scored on debut in Australia and had a run of 10 points finishes in 11 race weekends, though he has not scored in the last four events. In Sao Paulo he climbed from 18th to 11th.

Together the pair have Racing Bulls fifth in the constructors' championship on 114 points, 45 ahead of Alpine, per the BBC. Formula1.com says the Faenza team has never finished as high as fifth in its 20-year history.

Team principal Alan Permane said in the team's statement: "I have no doubt that this is the best driver line-up for us."

What happens to Tsolov

The team's release did not mention Tsolov, the BBC reported, and he is expected to stay with Red Bull in a reserve role with simulator and older-car testing. Tsolov drove for Racing Bulls in first practice in Mexico City and finished the session 12th, Formula1.com said. He led the F2 standings until the previous round, when Ferrari junior Rafa Camara moved ahead.

Analysis: Keeping Lawson gives Red Bull a known benchmark for Lindblad while Tsolov gains more seasoning. It also breaks from Red Bull's habit of moving drivers on quickly, a point the BBC made directly.

The rest of the 2027 grid

With Racing Bulls settled, Formula1.com says two seats still carry questions: Haas has not yet announced a new contract for Ollie Bearman, though both sides have said they want to continue, and Sergio Perez's place at Cadillac is less certain after test driver Colton Herta earned his Super Licence. For more on Bearman, see our story on his "horrible three weeks" at Haas.
