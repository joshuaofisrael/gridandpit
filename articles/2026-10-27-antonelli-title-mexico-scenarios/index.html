How Kimi Antonelli clinched the 2026 title in Mexico City

By Grid and Pit

Third place in Mexico City made Antonelli the 2026 champion. The points math below is what BBC and Sky published before the race.

Last updated November 20, 2026

Update, 20 November 2026: Antonelli finished third in Mexico City and clinched the title. Guinness World Records, checked 20 November 2026, records the third place and his age of 20 years and 68 days. The classification is in our Mexico City result. The paragraphs below are the math BBC Sport and Sky Sports published before the race. They are not a live scenario.

Before the race, Kimi Antonelli was champion in Mexico City if he finished ninth or better, or if neither George Russell nor Lewis Hamilton won. That is how BBC Sport and Sky Sports both laid it out after his win in Austin.

The starting point

After the United States Grand Prix, the drivers' standings showed Antonelli on 365 points, Russell on 242 and Hamilton on 241. That was a lead of 123 points over Russell and 124 over Hamilton. Those totals are the 25 October 2026 reading cited here. The live standings page has moved on.

Sky Sports said Max Verstappen, Charles Leclerc and Lando Norris could no longer win the title, which left Russell and Hamilton as the only drivers who could still stop Antonelli. After Mexico City, four races remained, Brazil, Las Vegas, Qatar and Abu Dhabi, with a maximum of 100 points available, according to Sky Sports.

The math

A win is worth 25 points. Antonelli needed to leave Mexico City with a lead larger than the 100 points still available afterward.

  • If Russell wins and scores 25, Antonelli kept a big enough lead with ninth place, which pays 2 points. The BBC said he was champion as long as he did not lose more than 23 points to Russell.
  • If Hamilton wins, Antonelli could lose up to 24 points to him, so tenth place, worth 1 point, was enough, the BBC said.
  • If neither Russell nor Hamilton wins, neither could gain enough, and Antonelli was champion wherever he finished.

In practice, Sky Sports noted, Russell or Hamilton would need to win all five remaining races while Antonelli failed to score two points for him to lose the title.

The constructors' title

Mercedes led Ferrari by 133 points and also had a first mathematical chance to clinch the teams' title in Mexico City, Sky Sports reported, though it called that a very tall order that would require Mercedes to outscore Ferrari by 39 points. Mercedes did not settle it in Mexico City. The team did it in Sao Paulo, in our constructors' title story.

The countback that made ninth enough is in the tiebreak explainer. His place on the age list is in youngest F1 world champions. Session times from that weekend remain in the Mexico City race times guide.

Update, 31 October: Antonelli was second in FP1 and fourth in FP2 behind Charles Leclerc. Read our Friday practice report.
