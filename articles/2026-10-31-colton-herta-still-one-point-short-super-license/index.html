Colton Herta is still one point short of a super license after Mexico City practice

By Grid and Pit

A correction to IndyCar's 2025 standings means Colton Herta is on 39 super license points, not 40, so Cadillac needs to run him in one more practice session this year.

Last updated October 31, 2026

Colton Herta went into first practice in Mexico City believing a clean run would give him the 40th and final point he needs for a Formula 1 super license. He completed the session in Valtteri Bottas's Cadillac, but he is still one point short, as The Race and the BBC both report.

Where the missing point went

The difference comes from his 2025 IndyCar finish. Herta ended that season level on points with Felix Rosenqvist, and Rosenqvist is classified ahead because he had more second place finishes. The Race says IndyCar's website had shown Herta sixth, ahead of Rosenqvist, until the listing was corrected this week to seventh. The official standings issued at the end of 2025 already had him seventh.

That matters because sixth in IndyCar is worth 6 super license points and seventh is worth 4. Cadillac had checked its numbers with the FIA in July based on the sixth place listing, according to The Race, which is why the team expected Mexico City to finish the job.

The updated count

Using the figures from The Race and Motorsport.com:

  1. 10th in IndyCar in 2023: 1 point
  2. 2nd in IndyCar in 2024: 30 points
  3. 7th in IndyCar in 2025: 4 points
  4. Four qualifying FP1 outings in 2026: 4 points

That totals 39. Each practice outing of at least 100 kilometers without penalty points adds one point, up to 10 in total, and those points count regardless of which year the session took place.

Where he could get the last point

Mexico City was Herta's fifth FP1 appearance of the season, but GrandPrix.com notes he completed only five laps at Monza, short of the 100 kilometer mark, so only four runs count. The same report says another outing is planned in Brazil. PlanetF1 quotes team principal Marcin Budkowski as saying Cadillac may run him in another FP1 before the season ends, and the BBC also says the team plans to do so. For US start times of the remaining sessions, see our guides to São Paulo and Las Vegas.

What it means for 2027

The license is a separate question from the seat. The BBC reports that Cadillac is considering Herta for a race seat in place of Bottas next season, while PlanetF1 reports that Bottas is understood to remain the preference as the team assesses its options. Cadillac has not announced its 2027 lineup.

Herta was 22nd in FP1 with a 1:21.365. In Cadillac's notes published by Formula 1, he said: "I always have a grin whenever I drive this car."
