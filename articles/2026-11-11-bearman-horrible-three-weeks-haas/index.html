Bearman calls Haas's last three races a 'horrible three weeks' after finishing 17th in Sao Paulo

By Grid and Pit

In an 11 November Formula1.com interview, Ollie Bearman said he just wanted to go home and reset after a Sao Paulo finish of 17th and two earlier retirements for Haas.

Last updated November 11, 2026

Ollie Bearman said he was glad to be heading home after a difficult three-race run for Haas, according to a Formula1.com article published at 10:35am UTC on 11 November. The official championship site said Bearman finished 17th in the Sao Paulo Grand Prix last Sunday, did not finish the races in Austin and Mexico City, and that slow pit stops in Brazil dropped him from the fringes of the points to the back of the field.

Formula1.com said Bearman had qualified in the top 10 in the wet in Sao Paulo, but that the race began with early contact with Fernando Alonso that sent both drivers wide. Bearman is quoted saying: "I had a bit of a moment at the start but nothing major, I lost a few places at the start but managed to gain back a few again, so was net zero. After that I just had some very slow pit stops and I couldn't fight with that." The article said that pit stop dropped him from 10th to 17th.

"I just want to go home and reset. It has been a horrible three weeks, we have missed two opportunities to score points, and we just need to move on and do better," Bearman said, according to the same article.

The official report recaps the earlier races in the triple-header. In Austin, it said Bearman made contact with Pierre Gasly, received a 10-second time penalty for causing a collision, and retired with damage. Bearman is quoted from the time calling it a racing incident and saying he felt "massively penalised" because the damage had already ruined his race. In Mexico City, the article said he was hit from behind by Nico Hulkenberg and forced to retire, and quotes Bearman saying he "just got smashed off" while turning into Turn 4 and was in the points at the time. It also said stand-in teammate Ryo Hirakawa was promoted to 10th from 13th after post-race penalties that day.

Formula1.com said the result left Haas with one point from the three-race stretch, while rivals Audi scored five points and closed the gap in the fight for seventh in the standings. Those championship figures are as stated in the official article.
