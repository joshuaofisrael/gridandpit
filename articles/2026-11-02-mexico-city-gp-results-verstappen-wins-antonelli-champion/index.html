Mexico City Grand Prix 2026 results: Verstappen wins as Antonelli becomes F1 champion

By Grid and Pit

Max Verstappen won the Mexico City Grand Prix by 0.799 seconds from Lewis Hamilton, but third place was enough for Kimi Antonelli to clinch the 2026 title, the youngest champion in F1 history. Full standings after Mexico City are inside.

Last updated November 2, 2026

Max Verstappen won the Mexico City Grand Prix on Sunday, finishing 0.799 seconds ahead of Lewis Hamilton, according to the official race result. Third place was enough for Kimi Antonelli to clinch the 2026 drivers' title, BBC Sport reported, so there is a winner of the race and a separate winner of the championship on the same afternoon at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez.

It was Verstappen's third win of the season and his third in the last four Grands Prix, Formula 1's own race report says, a run that Autosport also notes. Antonelli finished third, 4.006 seconds back, enough to seal the title with four rounds still to run.

How Sunday played out

The race was stopped under red flag after Cadillac's Valtteri Bottas crashed, and the field restarted from a standing start, the official Formula 1 report says. A late Safety Car followed a collision between Arvid Lindblad and Oscar Piastri, which bunched the field again before the restart, BBC Sport reported.

Ferrari's Charles Leclerc led early after passing Hamilton on the opening lap, but he served a five second penalty during his pit stop, which handed the lead to Verstappen, Formula 1's report says. Autosport called it a day when Ferrari threw away its own chance of victory while Verstappen made the most of the chaos around him.

Pirelli's post race report says Verstappen ran a two stop strategy, Medium tires to a new set of Softs on lap 27, then another Soft after the red flag, and that he held off Hamilton's late pressure to the finish, according to Pirelli. Antonelli started on Softs, switched to Mediums on lap 43 and fitted a used set of Softs at the restart, a strategy Pirelli said was enough to secure third place and the title.

Full classification and penalties

The top ten finishers, by the official classification, also confirmed by the FIA's own results page:

Three drivers were penalized after the race. Oscar Piastri was given a ten second penalty for causing a collision, and both Franco Colapinto and Pierre Gasly were penalized for failing to slow under yellow flags, the official result page notes. None of the three scored points once the penalties were applied, which is also reflected in the FIA classification.

Antonelli clinches the title

Antonelli went into the race needing only ninth place to become champion, regardless of what Hamilton or his Mercedes teammate George Russell did, Formula 1's report says. He started fourth and finished third, enough to seal the crown with four rounds to spare, according to both Formula 1 and the AP wire report.

At 20 years old, Antonelli is the youngest champion in Formula 1 history, beating the record Sebastian Vettel set in 2010, and the first Italian champion since Alberto Ascari in 1953, both BBC Sport and Autosport report. "Thank you so much. We did it. We did it," Antonelli said on his slowing down lap, BBC Sport reported, a radio message also captured in the FIA's post race transcript.

Standings after Mexico City

Antonelli's point total jumps from 365 before the weekend to 380 after it, well clear of the field, the official drivers' standings show. Behind him, Verstappen's win moves him up to second place in the championship, two points ahead of Hamilton, according to both Formula 1 and Crash.net.

George Russell drops from second to fourth in the table after finishing sixth in Mexico City, while Charles Leclerc moves up to fifth, both the official standings and Crash.net show. Our fight for second piece from before the race named these same four drivers as the ones to watch for the runner up spot, and that is exactly how it has played out.

In the constructors' table, Mercedes leads on 630 points to Ferrari's 504, with Red Bull Racing third on 382, the official team standings and Crash.net both confirm.

Mercedes did not quite wrap up the constructors' title in Mexico City, and Crash.net notes the team is now close enough that it could be settled at the next round in Sao Paulo. Cadillac, the American team, has yet to score a point this season, the same standings page confirms.
