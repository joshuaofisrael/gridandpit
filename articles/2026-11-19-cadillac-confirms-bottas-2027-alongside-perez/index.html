"To whom it may concern, I'm staying": Cadillac keeps Bottas for 2027, ending weeks of doubt

By Grid and Pit

Cadillac has confirmed Valtteri Bottas will race for the team again in 2027 alongside Sergio Pérez. CEO Dan Towriss pointed to his experience and the value of continuity for a team still building its car.

Last updated November 19, 2026

Cadillac's 2027 Formula 1 lineup is settled. Valtteri Bottas will stay with the team next season, SB Nation reports, ending weeks of speculation days before the Las Vegas Grand Prix. The team's announcement opened with the line: "To whom it may concern, I'm staying."

What Bottas said

"I'm excited to continue this journey with Cadillac into 2027," Bottas said in the announcement, as reported by SB Nation. "We knew building a new Formula 1 team from the ground up wasn't going to be easy, but that's exactly what makes this challenge so special."

He added: "We've laid the foundations, we're learning and improving, and there's still plenty more to come, I'm hungry for more, and I believe in what we're building together. The story is only just getting started."

Why Cadillac kept him

Cadillac F1 CEO Dan Towriss pointed to Bottas's experience. "Valtteri's experience, perspective and commitment are important strengths for our team," he said. "Continuity gives us a strong platform as we continue developing the team and working toward our ambitions in Formula 1."

Towriss said everyone in the team was pleased to have Bottas "locked in for 2027 alongside Checo".

The full lineup

Cadillac had already extended Sergio Pérez's contract before the Mexico City Grand Prix, so Bottas's seat was the open question. It was still unresolved when he missed media day in Mexico City last month. Both drivers now carry on into the team's second season.
