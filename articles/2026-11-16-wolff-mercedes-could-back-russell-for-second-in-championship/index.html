Wolff says Mercedes could throw their weight behind Russell in the fight for second place

By Grid and Pit

With both titles won, Toto Wolff says Mercedes want George Russell to finish runner-up to Kimi Antonelli. Russell heads to Las Vegas third, 17 points behind Max Verstappen and one ahead of Lewis Hamilton.

Last updated November 16, 2026

Mercedes could put George Russell first over the last three races of the season to help him finish second in the drivers' championship, team principal Toto Wolff has said, according to Sky Sports.

The standings

Kimi Antonelli won the drivers' title in Mexico and Mercedes sealed the constructors' championship a week later in Brazil. Russell has not won since the Azerbaijan Grand Prix in September and has dropped to third. Sky Sports lists Max Verstappen second on 279 points, Russell third on 262 and Lewis Hamilton fourth on 261, with Charles Leclerc on 249 and Lando Norris on 246.

What Wolff said

Speaking after the Sao Paulo Grand Prix, Wolff said Mercedes had not yet thought about the drivers' standings but definitely want Russell to score the maximum and finish second. He added that giving Russell full backing "could be a possibility". He also said he expects Russell, whose best championship finish is fourth, to recover strongly in 2027.

Looking ahead

Wolff warned that Mercedes have won only one of the last five races, Antonelli's United States Grand Prix, and that Ferrari, McLaren and Red Bull will all be in next year's title fight. He said the team will use the last three rounds to learn for next year's car and power unit while still racing flat out.

The run-in starts at the Las Vegas Grand Prix this weekend, followed by Qatar and Abu Dhabi.

Our read

Analysis: team orders in the fight for second are now an option for Mercedes, as Antonelli has nothing left to win. The margins are small, though: one bad weekend could drop Russell behind Hamilton.
