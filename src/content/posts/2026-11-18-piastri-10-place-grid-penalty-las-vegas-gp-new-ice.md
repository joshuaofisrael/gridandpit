Oscar Piastri is set to start this weekend's Las Vegas Grand Prix 10 places lower than he qualifies. [Formula 1 posted on X](XF1) on Wednesday morning that the McLaren driver "is set to take a 10-place grid penalty at this weekend's Las Vegas Grand Prix".

## Why he is being penalised

According to the [full story on formula1.com](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/piastri-set-for-10-place-grid-penalty-at-las-vegas-grand-prix.5eT1wWFQzCfOS6W2tvTtLk), a reliability issue has forced McLaren to switch Piastri to a new internal combustion engine (ICE). That is his fifth of the season, one more than the allowance of four, which triggers the penalty. It is the first time this year he has gone over the limit on that component.

McLaren believes Piastri "has a good chance to recover positions with overtaking" on a track that offers several passing opportunities.

## Turbo upgrade for both cars

Piastri and Lando Norris will both take a new turbocharger in Las Vegas as Mercedes, McLaren's power unit supplier, brings an upgrade through the Additional Development and Upgrade Opportunities (ADUO) mechanism. Neither driver is penalised for that change because both remain within their allowances. McLaren says the expected performance gain is "relatively small".

## More parts on the car

The team is also bringing more aerodynamic upgrades, led by a new floor, to add to the package it introduced in Brazil around a new front wing concept. McLaren says it has "some solutions to improve drag" to try in practice before deciding whether to race them. It describes this late-season work as feeding into its 2027 car.

## What is at stake

McLaren are fighting Red Bull for third in the Teams' Championship and trail them by 17 points with a maximum of 129 still available, Formula 1 says. A 10-place drop for one of their cars makes that chase harder this weekend.
