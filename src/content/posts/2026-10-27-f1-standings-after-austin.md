Kimi Antonelli leads the 2026 drivers' championship with 365 points after the United States Grand Prix, 123 ahead of Mercedes teammate George Russell on 242, according to the [official drivers' standings](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2026/drivers). Mercedes leads the constructors' table with 607 points, ahead of Ferrari on 474, the [official team standings](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2026/team) show.

Only Russell and Hamilton can still mathematically beat Antonelli, [Sky Sports reports](https://www.skysports.com/f1/news/13603683/kimi-antonelli-f1-title-permutations-how-mercedes-driver-can-win-2026-formula-1-drivers-championship-at-mexico-city-gp-on-sunday). The gap from first to second, 123 points, is Grid and Pit arithmetic from the official table.
