Robert Shwartzman will make his Formula E debut with Lola in the 2027 season, [Formula E announced](https://www.fiaformulae.com/en/news/teams/robert-shwartzman-joins-lola-formula-e-for-gen4) on Wednesday, as the series moves to its new Gen4 car.

[RACER reported](https://racer.com/2026/11/10/lola-formula-e-team-signs-shwartzman-as-first-driver-for-2027-season) that Shwartzman is the first driver Lola has named for the season. The team needs a new line-up after Lucas di Grassi retired and Zane Maloney moved to Nissan.

## Who Shwartzman is

The 27-year-old won the first FIA Formula 3 title in 2019 and was runner-up in Formula 2 in 2021, according to [Formula E](https://www.fiaformulae.com/en/news/teams/robert-shwartzman-joins-lola-formula-e-for-gen4). He was a member of the Ferrari Driver Academy from 2017 to 2022 and served as a Formula 1 test and reserve driver, taking part in practice sessions. He later raced sports cars, including in the World Endurance Championship, before moving to IndyCar with PREMA Racing.

RACER noted that he took pole for the 2025 Indianapolis 500 and that he will race in the WEC with Peugeot alongside his Formula E program. He did not race in 2026, and said in the team's statement that he had missed racing a great deal.

Shwartzman has already been testing Lola's T004 car with technical partner Yamaha, [Formula E said](https://www.fiaformulae.com/en/news/teams/robert-shwartzman-joins-lola-formula-e-for-gen4). He also tested for the former DS Penske team at the 2023 and 2024 Berlin rookie tests, [per RACER](https://racer.com/2026/11/10/lola-formula-e-team-signs-shwartzman-as-first-driver-for-2027-season).

## What Lola said

Team principal Mark Preston said Shwartzman enters as a rookie facing a real learning curve against experienced drivers, but has shown he can adapt quickly across categories, according to Formula E.

**Context:** The Gen4 season now starts in Mexico City on 16 January after Formula E moved its Jeddah opener to April. See our story on the [revised calendar](/articles/2026-11-12-formula-e-moves-jeddah-mexico-city-opens-gen4-season/).
