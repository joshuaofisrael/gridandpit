Update, 21 November 2026: the [records page](/records/) copies five ages from a Formula 1 article published 3 November 2026. That article prints Vettel at 23 years and 133 days and Alonso at 24 years and 57 days. The table below still follows the Wikipedia list checked on 20 November, which prints 134 and 58. This page does not choose between those day counts.

Update, 20 November 2026: Antonelli is the champion. He finished third in Mexico City on 1 November 2026. [Guinness World Records](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/youngest-formula-one-world-champion), checked 20 November 2026, lists him at 20 years and 68 days, born 25 August 2006. The [Wikipedia list of champions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Formula_One_World_Drivers%27_Champions), checked the same day, puts him first on the youngest list. Our [Mexico City result](/articles/2026-11-02-mexico-city-gp-results-verstappen-wins-antonelli-champion/) has the classification.

The page below was written while the title was still open. The ages in the table are the current youngest ten, not a forecast.

## How old he was

Antonelli was born on 25 August 2006, which both [Guinness](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/youngest-formula-one-world-champion) and his [Mercedes profile](https://www.mercedesamgf1.com/drivers/driver/andrea-kimi-antonelli) give, the profile placing the birth in Bologna. From 25 August to 1 November is 68 days. That is the same figure Guinness publishes for the record.

He was more than three years younger than Sebastian Vettel, who had held the mark at 23 years and 134 days.

## The youngest champions

These are the youngest drivers to win a first title. Ages are from the [Wikipedia youngest champions table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Formula_One_World_Drivers%27_Champions), checked 20 November 2026. [Guinness](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/youngest-formula-one-world-champion) matches Antonelli's line: 20 years and 68 days, at the Mexico City Grand Prix. Vettel's 23 years and 134 days is the same figure Guinness used for the previous record, and it is still the second line on the Wikipedia table.

Jim Clark, at 27 years and 188 days in 1963, was tenth on this list before Antonelli won. He is no longer inside the ten. This check did not reopen a source for his new rank, so the page does not assign him one.

## Italy's wait is over

Before the race, Reuters said a title would make Antonelli Italy's first champion in 73 years. The last before him was Alberto Ascari, who won in 1952 and 1953, which [Wikipedia's champions list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Formula_One_World_Drivers%27_Champions) still shows, along with Giuseppe Farina in 1950. The same list now includes Antonelli for 2026. Reuters also noted nine wins that season before Mexico City, including Monza, where he became the first Italian winner there since 1966. Guinness, checked 20 November 2026, records nine wins at the moment he became champion.

"It would be very cool to close this off, to be the next Italian champion, but of course then this has to be just the beginning," Antonelli told Reuters in Austin, before the race.

## The American comparison

Only two Americans have won the drivers' title, both on the [Wikipedia list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Formula_One_World_Drivers%27_Champions): Phil Hill in 1961 and Mario Andretti in 1978. STATS F1 listed their ages as 34 and 38 when this page was first checked on 28 October 2026. Those two ages were not opened again on 20 November. Antonelli's 20 years and 68 days, from Guinness that day, is far younger than either figure.
