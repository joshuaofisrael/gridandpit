Lewis Hamilton's final practice at Interlagos ended early on Saturday after he reported smoke in the cockpit of his Ferrari and headed straight for the pits.

Formula 1 [posted the moment on X](https://x.com/F1/status/2119084519039144262) during the session:

> "Hamilton returns to the pits after reporting smoke in the cockpit. Looks like Lewis' FP3 is over with around 25 mins of the session to run."

Hamilton did not return to the track. He completed only seven laps, all on the medium tyre, and finished the session 12th with a 1:12.833, according to [Autosport's FP3 report](https://www.autosport.com/f1/news/f1-brazilian-gp-verstappen-edges-out-antonelli-to-top-fp3-as-hamilton-hits-mechanical-trouble/10872582/). He and Aston Martin's Lance Stroll were the only drivers who did not set a lap on the soft tyre.

## A new engine, then more trouble

The problem came on a weekend when Hamilton's car had already been fitted with a fresh internal combustion engine, and he is among several drivers taking grid penalties for power unit parts at the Sao Paulo Grand Prix. Earlier in the session Autosport reported that Hamilton complained of "strange vibrations" from the new powertrain and "big engine braking" before the smoke was reported.

Ferrari had not said what caused the smoke by the end of the session. Whether the issue is linked to the new engine, and whether it affects his qualifying run, is still to be confirmed.

## Verstappen leads the final practice

Max Verstappen set the fastest time of the session, a 1:11.622 on the soft tyre, just 0.025 seconds ahead of Kimi Antonelli. Lando Norris was third, 0.131 seconds off, with George Russell fourth and Charles Leclerc fifth, so the four leading teams were covered by 0.274 seconds.

Antonelli had an early steering wheel problem and was called in for a replacement, [Motorsport Week reported](https://www.motorsportweek.com/2026/11/07/max-verstappen-fastest-brazil-fp3/). Stroll's session ended about 15 minutes in with a suspected ERS issue.

Dark clouds were building over Interlagos as the session finished, so rain could still play a part in qualifying.
