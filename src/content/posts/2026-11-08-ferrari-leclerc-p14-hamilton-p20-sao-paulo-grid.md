Ferrari have confirmed both of their cars start the Sao Paulo Grand Prix well down the order after power unit penalties.

The team [posted a starting-grid graphic](https://x.com/ScuderiaFerrari/status/2119399541661401256) on race morning, 8 November 2026: "Here's where LH and CL start for today's São Paulo GP, after penalties were applied." The graphic shows Charles Leclerc in P14 and Lewis Hamilton in P20.

## Why both Ferraris dropped

Formula 1 [reported on 7 November](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/hamilton-leclerc-and-verstappen-set-for-sao-paulo-grand-prix-penalties-after-power-unit-changes.7B5CuFFCN1F6Txc8YwcEhd) that Ferrari had gone over the season allowance for the internal combustion engine on both cars, which carries a 10-place drop. Hamilton's new engine then ran into problems in final practice, and Ferrari changed his whole power unit, which added further penalties and a back-of-the-grid start.

[BBC Sport](https://www.bbc.com/sport/formula1/articles/c574p28devp4o) said Leclerc qualified fourth before his 10-place penalty. Hamilton's 20th place puts him ahead of only the other cars sent to the back, Max Verstappen's Red Bull and Sergio Perez's Cadillac, according to the same reports.

## What it means for the race

Ferrari now need recovery drives from both cars. BBC Sport noted that Leclerc, Hamilton, Verstappen and George Russell are separated by just 16 points in the fight for second in the drivers' championship behind Kimi Antonelli, so places gained on Sunday matter.

The grid positions are Ferrari's [race-morning post](https://x.com/ScuderiaFerrari/status/2119399541661401256); the reasons are set out in the Formula 1 and BBC reports.
