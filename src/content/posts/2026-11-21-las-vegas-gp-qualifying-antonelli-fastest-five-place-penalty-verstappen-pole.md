The fastest man in Las Vegas will not start at the front. "Ride onboard with Kimi Antonelli as he sets the fastest time in qualifying," [Formula 1 posted on X](https://x.com/F1/status/2124071821322407969). "Due to a five-place grid penalty the Italian will start from P6, with Max Verstappen inheriting pole."

Red Bull did not hide its delight. "On Pole for the first time in Vegas," the team [posted](https://x.com/redbullracing/status/2124057335261712537).

## How the grid shapes up

The world champion's lap was a 1m 33.893s, 0.258s clear of Verstappen, according to the [Formula1.com qualifying report](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/antonelli-quickest-in-las-vegas-gp-qualifying-as-verstappen-inherits-pole-position.6R7udh8iYMHunSOGI6zStK). The penalty is for power unit changes. With Antonelli moved back, George Russell joins Verstappen on the front row, and Lando Norris and Isack Hadjar share row two.

Arvid Lindblad starts fifth, ahead of Antonelli. Oscar Piastri qualified seventh but drops to 17th with a 10-place penalty of his own, so Liam Lawson, Pierre Gasly and Franco Colapinto each move up a place. Carlos Sainz and Ollie Bearman also have power unit penalties.

## A lap saved by the tow

Antonelli nearly did not get a final run. Drivers jostled for slipstream position on their out laps, and he "only just" made it onto his last flying lap, Formula1.com says. "It was a very weird Qualifying for me," he said. "I couldn't get the tyres working, just zero feeling with the car, and also the brake pedal was very long." His last lap began a second behind the car in front: "I thought I was screwed... But the tow is so big here that I was able to gain with that."

Verstappen set his time on the first Q3 runs with a tow down the back straight and did not improve on his final effort. It still puts him first on the grid.

## Tonight

Teams had only one dry session before qualifying after Thursday's washout, and track temperature was just 15C at the start. The Las Vegas Grand Prix starts at 20:00 local time on Saturday, according to Formula1.com. Ferrari qualified only 11th and 12th after both cars went out in Q2, and Mercedes will need Russell to fight Verstappen from the front row while Antonelli recovers from sixth.
