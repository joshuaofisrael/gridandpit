Kimi Antonelli says his preparations for the 2027 Formula 1 season begin with this weekend's Sao Paulo Grand Prix, his first race since securing the world championship in Mexico City.

The Mercedes driver clinched the title last weekend in Mexico City, becoming the youngest world champion in Formula 1 history, according to [BBC Sport](https://www.bbc.com/sport/formula1/articles/cx0544g2n1jdo). Antonelli is 20 and achieved the championship in only his second season.

## Building for next year

Rather than treating the remaining races as a wind down after the title has been decided, Antonelli said he wants to finish the campaign strongly. The BBC report says he sees the closing rounds as an opportunity to build confidence ahead of his attempt to retain the championship.

The Sao Paulo event also carries a particular visual tribute. Antonelli is due to wear a helmet design inspired by Ayrton Senna, the Brazilian driver, for the race at Interlagos. The BBC report notes the design as a tribute to the legendary Brazilian.

## What comes next

Antonelli's comments put the focus on momentum and preparation rather than celebration alone. A strong finish would give the champion a chance to close his title winning season with further results, while beginning the work of adapting to the demands of another championship campaign.

The immediate challenge is the Sao Paulo weekend, where Antonelli returns to the track as the reigning champion for the first time. The longer task is to turn a breakthrough second season into sustained success.
