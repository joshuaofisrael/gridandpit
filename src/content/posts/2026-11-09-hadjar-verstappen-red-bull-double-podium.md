Red Bull team principal Laurent Mekies says Max Verstappen's climb from 21st on the grid to second in the Sao Paulo Grand Prix was beyond anything the team's simulations predicted. Isack Hadjar finished third to give Red Bull a double podium. Mekies explained the result to [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/mekies-explains-how-verstappen-outperformed-red-bulls-own-simulations-in-brazil.1ps87tyvjdEf23vDCaDXOZ).

"No sim in the world would predict that you go from P21 to P2, or that you get 14 places in five laps," Mekies said. "So he's beating the sim on a regular basis!"

## An unplanned penalty

Verstappen started at the back after power-unit components were changed on his car. Mekies said this was not part of the plan. "It was not planned. We had a small issue on Friday that forced us to do so," he said, adding that this was the team's first penalty with Verstappen in the first year with the new power unit.

## McLaren were faster

Mekies did not claim Verstappen would have won from the front. "I also think that even if you start P2, say, I think on pace Lando was anyway a bit faster than us," he said.

He said Red Bull still needs to work out why. Verstappen was "very, very unhappy with car balance" from the switch to dry tires onward, including after the late change back to intermediates. "Is it degradation? Is it pure pace?" Mekies said, noting that McLaren had brought "another fair bit of upgrade" to the race.

## Why Hadjar did not get P2 back

Hadjar was running second midway through the race but let Verstappen past after running wide at Turn 1. F1's race report says Hadjar later asked whether the positions would be reversed. Mekies said Red Bull would have swapped the cars back "if the cars had stayed close together," but the gap grew and the rain got heavier at the end, so "it would not have made sense."

Red Bull split the strategy. Verstappen pitted at the end of Lap 45, while Hadjar said he could run to the end before the team brought him in a few laps later. He finished 24.485 seconds behind Norris and 9.883 seconds behind Verstappen.

## "Raised the bar"

Mekies called it Hadjar's best race for Red Bull "in terms of pure pace," on both dry and intermediate tires. He said this kind of progress is what persuaded the team to confirm Hadjar for 2027. F1 says this was Red Bull's second double podium of the season, after Baku.

Mekies also said the four top teams are closer than expected: "The convergence is probably happening earlier than what we all thought."
