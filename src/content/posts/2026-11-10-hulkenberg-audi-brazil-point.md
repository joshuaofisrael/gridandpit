Nico Hulkenberg called tenth place in the Sao Paulo Grand Prix "the maximum possible" after recovering from a 10 second penalty to score the last point on offer for Audi, [Formula 1 reported](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/the-maximum-possible-hulkenberg-on-audis-fight-for-points-in-sao-paulo-grand-prix.1KO1iL7LzQm3Cfl0CGspws).

## From fourth on the grid

Hulkenberg had starred in wet qualifying and lined up fourth for the 71 lap race after grid penalties for George Russell and Charles Leclerc moved him up two places. As the early wet conditions dried, the Audi slipped back and ended up fighting Franco Colapinto's Alpine on the edge of the points.

He got past Colapinto at Turn 1 but only after leaving the track, which earned him a 10 second penalty. He served it at a late stop when drivers pitted for intermediate tyres during another shower, which dropped him to eleventh.

## The late pass on Lindblad

Hulkenberg then chased down Arvid Lindblad and passed the Racing Bulls driver seven laps from the end. He said he set up the move on corner entry to get a better exit, and that it felt good to win the place back after the penalty. He added that Audi lacked pace on both slicks and intermediates and would review the weekend.

The point was only Hulkenberg's fifth top ten finish in a Grand Prix this season.

## Bortoleto's difficult home race

Team mate Gabriel Bortoleto finished sixteenth at his home race after crashing early in qualifying left him nineteenth on the grid. He said his pace was positive whenever he found clear air, but overtaking was hard because power is limited to 250 kW in the rain, leaving little top speed advantage on the straights.

## The standings picture

The result cut Audi's deficit to Haas in the fight for seventh in the constructors' championship to three points. With the field this close in the midfield, a single point can decide a place in the final table, and with it a share of prize money.
