Lando Norris took pole position for the Sao Paulo Grand Prix after a rain-soaked, heavily delayed qualifying session at Interlagos on Saturday.

Formula 1 [confirmed the result on X](https://x.com/F1/status/2119164887972884545):

> "LANDO NORRIS TAKES POLE IN SAO PAULO! 🇧🇷👊"

The McLaren driver set a 1:27.470 in Q3, 0.204 seconds clear of Mercedes' George Russell, according to [Formula1.com](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/norris-beats-russell-to-pole-position-in-dramatic-rain-affected-sao-paolo-qualifying.2V60rhlQjdS5my1uFrOKUH). It is his third consecutive pole at Interlagos.

## Penalties reshape the front of the grid

The qualifying order is not the starting order. Russell has a five-place grid penalty for exceeding his allocation of power unit parts, so his team-mate and newly crowned world champion Kimi Antonelli, who qualified third, moves up to second, [BBC Sport reported](https://www.bbc.com/sport/formula1/articles/c6gell5l7l3zo).

Ferrari's Charles Leclerc qualified fourth but carries a 10-place penalty for a new internal combustion engine, which [ESPN said](https://www.espn.com/f1/story/_/id/50389061/lando-norris-sao-paulo-grand-prix-brazil-formula-1) drops him to 14th. That promotes Racing Bulls' Liam Lawson from fifth to third.

The wet conditions also rewarded the midfield. Audi's Nico Hulkenberg qualified sixth and will start fourth, Haas driver Oliver Bearman was eighth and will start sixth, and Fernando Alonso and Alex Albon completed the top 10. Lewis Hamilton (11th) and Max Verstappen (16th) already faced power unit penalties that send them to the back.

## A long wait for running

Q1 was red-flagged less than three minutes in when a torrential downpour hit the circuit, and the session did not restart until an hour and 24 minutes later, ESPN reported. Russell was frustrated in Q3 after his team told him to abandon a lap that he believed would have been quicker before the rain worsened.

"It's been a long day," Norris said, per Formula1.com. "It was about getting two laps in at the beginning and not making any mistakes, and that's exactly what we did, so it's been a very good day."

Sunday's race at Interlagos is round 20 of the 2026 championship.
