Alisha Palmowski, who won the 2026 F1 Academy title in Austin, will join the Red Bull Junior Team next year and step up to the Formula Regional European Championship (FREC) with Rodin Motorsport. [Formula1.com reported](F1_AP) on Friday that the seat is fully funded, with Red Bull and F1 Academy supporting her 2027 campaign together.

## The deal

[Red Bull says](https://www.redbullracing.com/int-en/alisha-palmowski-joins-red-bull-junior-team) Palmowski will race for Rodin in FREC while getting the support of its junior programme, and Formula1.com adds that she will have a comprehensive testing programme. Red Bull notes that the junior route is the one taken by Max Verstappen, Sebastian Vettel, Daniel Ricciardo and Isack Hadjar.

Before then she will finish her time with the Red Bull Racing Academy Programme at next week's F1 Academy finale in Las Vegas. She goes there with 172 points. Red Bull puts her lead at 53 points, while [F1 Academy's own release](F1A_AP) says 52; either way, the title is already hers.

## Two seasons in the series

The 20-year-old Briton first raced in F1 Academy as a wild card in Qatar in 2024. That earned her a place in the Red Bull Racing Academy Programme and a Campos Racing seat for 2025, when she won on her debut weekend in Shanghai and finished fifth. This season she took five wins, seven podiums and six poles, and sealed the title with three races to spare, according to Formula1.com.

She is the third F1 Academy champion to move up the single-seater ladder, after Marta Garcia went to FREC and Abbi Pulling to GB3. In January she will also drive at the Race of Champions in New Zealand, partnering David Coulthard for Team GB.

Mekies said Red Bull was "very happy to keep Alisha in the Red Bull family". Palmowski said moving up the ladder had always been the goal. F1 Academy's own 2027 plans are in [our calendar story](/articles/2026-11-11-f1-academy-unveils-2027-calendar/).
