Lewis Hamilton said Ferrari gathered plenty of useful data on Friday at Interlagos and would work through it overnight ahead of qualifying.

Scuderia Ferrari [posted his comments](https://x.com/ScuderiaFerrari/status/2118833305734799529) after a busy Sao Paulo Friday:

> "We’ve gathered plenty of useful data, so we’ll work through that with the team tonight to understand where we can improve the car ahead of qualifying tomorrow."

## Setup work, not a grid claim

The quote is a driver assessment of Friday running, not a prediction of pole or race pace. Ferrari used the post to close its Friday coverage as Hamilton continues his 400th Grand Prix weekend. Until Saturday's sessions, treat the message as confirmation that the garage priority is overnight correlation of Friday data.

Primary source: Ferrari's [6 November post](https://x.com/ScuderiaFerrari/status/2118833305734799529).
