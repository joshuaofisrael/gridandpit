The fight for third in the constructors' championship just got close. [Formula 1 posted on X](https://x.com/F1/status/2124427918315344151) after the Las Vegas Grand Prix that McLaren are now nine points behind Red Bull with two rounds remaining.

## How the gap closed

Max Verstappen won in Las Vegas, but he was Red Bull's only scorer. According to [Autosport's results](https://www.autosport.com/f1/results/2026/las-vegas-gp-661701/), Isack Hadjar finished 15th, outside the points.

McLaren had both cars on the podium. Lando Norris was second and Oscar Piastri third, worth 18 and 15 points. That 33-point haul against Red Bull's 25 cut the gap by eight.

## The standings now

The [official team standings](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2026/team) after Las Vegas read:

- Mercedes: 666
- Ferrari: 517
- Red Bull: 440
- McLaren: 431
- Racing Bulls: 114
- Alpine: 87
- Haas: 31
- Audi: 28
- Williams: 16
- Aston Martin: 7
- Cadillac: 0

Mercedes lead Ferrari by 149 points. The more open battle is now the one for third, where a single strong weekend from either team could decide it.

## Why third place matters

Constructors' positions decide how prize money is shared among the teams, so each place up the table is worth real money. For McLaren, finishing ahead of Red Bull would also be a sign that their late-season form is real. For Red Bull, Verstappen is scoring heavily, but they need points from the second car to hold on.

## What comes next

The next round is the Qatar Grand Prix at the Lusail International Circuit on 27-29 November, followed by the season finale in Abu Dhabi. With both McLarens scoring and Red Bull relying mostly on one driver, the second Red Bull seat is the number to watch in Qatar.
