Nikola Tsolov will be the reserve driver for both Oracle Red Bull Racing and Visa Cash App Racing Bulls in 2027. Red Bull confirmed the role [on its website](https://www.redbullracing.com/int-en/nikola-tsolov-reserve-driver-2027) and [in a post on X](https://x.com/redbullracing/status/2120894951974224377) on Thursday, the same day Racing Bulls said [Liam Lawson and Arvid Lindblad would stay](/articles/2026-11-12-racing-bulls-keep-lawson-lindblad-2027/) as its race drivers.

## What the role involves

Red Bull says the 19-year-old Bulgarian will follow an extensive development programme that combines simulator and development work with track time in Free Practice sessions, Testing of Previous Cars (TPC), selected test sessions and tyre testing. The team says the programme is meant to make sure he is fully ready to race in F1.

As [Autosport notes](https://www.autosport.com/f1/news/tsolov-lands-reserve-driver-role-for-f1-2027/10874151/), the reserve job also means he would be the driver called on to substitute if a race driver at either team could not take part.

## How he got here

Tsolov joined the Red Bull Junior Team in 2025 and finished runner-up in that year's Formula 3 championship. In his first full Formula 2 season he has won six races, four Feature Races and two Sprints, and became the first driver in F2 history to win three in a row. He is second in the standings with two rounds left, in Qatar and Abu Dhabi.

[The Race reports](https://www.the-race.com/formula-1/tsolov-gets-twin-f1-2027-reserve-role-to-prepare-him-for-race-seat/) that he is fighting for the F2 title with Rafael Camara and Alex Dunne, and that his first practice outing for Racing Bulls in Mexico City compared well with Lindblad's.

Team principal Laurent Mekies called him "an exceptionally talented young driver". Tsolov said his ambition is to become an F1 driver and that he wants to be fully ready when the time comes.

## What it means

Red Bull has no race seat for Tsolov in 2027, but it has kept its top junior close to both teams. A year of practice sessions and older-car running is the same kind of route several drivers have taken before a full-time seat, and his F2 finish in Qatar and Abu Dhabi will add to his case for 2028.
