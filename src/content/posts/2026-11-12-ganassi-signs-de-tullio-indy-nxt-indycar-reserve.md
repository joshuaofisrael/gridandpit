Chip Ganassi Racing has signed Alessandro de Tullio to drive its No. 10 entry in the 2027 Indy NXT season, [INDYCAR.com reported](https://www.indycar.com/news/2026/11/11-11-detullio-ganassi-news) on Wednesday. The 20-year-old Argentine will also be the team's reserve driver for IndyCar road and street course weekends.

## A rookie year built on qualifying

De Tullio finished sixth in the 2026 Indy NXT standings as a rookie, with two wins, at Barber Motorsports Park and Portland, four podiums and five top-five finishes in 17 races, per [INDYCAR.com](https://www.indycar.com/news/2026/11/11-11-detullio-ganassi-news). He led all rookies with 99 laps led.

His eight poles were one short of the series single-season record and tied for the second most in Indy NXT history. INDYCAR.com credits the season to AJ Foyt Racing, while [RACER said](https://racer.com/2026/11/11/ganassi-signs-de-tullio-to-indy-nxt-race-seat-and-indycar-reserve-role) the car was run by HMD Motorsports under the AJ Foyt Racing banner.

## The reserve role

As IndyCar reserve, de Tullio will do simulator work and at-track support, according to INDYCAR.com. He completed several test sessions with Ganassi before signing. "Joining the most successful team in IndyCar is something that's very special," he said.

Ganassi has won a record 18 IndyCar championships, including six of the last seven through Scott Dixon and Alex Palou, but has never won an Indy NXT race, [INDYCAR.com noted](https://www.indycar.com/news/2026/11/11-11-detullio-ganassi-news). RACER described the team's four-car Indy NXT effort in 2026 as falling short of expectations.

Team manager Taylor Kiel said the goal is to give de Tullio and his teammates what they need to win races and the Indy NXT title. Carson Etter returns for 2027, and the team has yet to name drivers for its other entries.

**Why it matters:** A reserve seat at Ganassi puts de Tullio closest in line if one of its IndyCar regulars misses a road or street race, while his qualifying pace makes him an early name to watch in the 2027 Indy NXT title fight.
