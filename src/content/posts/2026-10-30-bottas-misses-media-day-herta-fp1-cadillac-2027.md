Valtteri Bottas did not take part in Thursday's media day at the Mexico City Grand Prix. In a team statement [published by GPblog](https://www.gpblog.com/en/breaking-news/valtteri-bottas-joins-kimi-antonelli-in-missing-mexico-media-duties-through-illness), Cadillac said Bottas was unwell and that it had decided "to allow him a day of rest, to support his recovery," adding: "This is a precautionary measure and does not currently affect his participation in the remainder of the race weekend." The team said it would continue to monitor the situation and give updates if needed.

Bottas was not the only absentee. Championship leader Kimi Antonelli also missed Thursday's media duties. A Mercedes spokesperson [told RACER](https://racer.com/2026/10/29/antonelli-bottas-skip-mexico-city-media-day-due-to-illness) that Antonelli was "under the weather" and recovering from a viral illness, that the FIA had excused him as a precaution, and that "we expect him to be back tomorrow." Antonelli can clinch the title this weekend, as our [title scenarios guide](/articles/2026-10-27-antonelli-title-mexico-scenarios/) explains.

## Herta in the car for first practice

Bottas was already due to sit out first practice. Cadillac said in its [race preview](https://www.cadillacf1team.com/news/the-cadillac-formula-1-r-team-aim-to-reach-new-heights-in-mexico-city) that Colton Herta would drive Bottas's car in the opening session, his fifth Friday outing for the team. Herta needs 100 kilometers, about 24 laps, to secure the final point he needs for a super license. We covered that in our [Herta super license story](/articles/2026-10-28-colton-herta-super-licence-points-mexico/).

## The 2027 question

Sergio Perez has extended his deal to at least the end of 2029, so one Cadillac seat is settled. The other is not. In a [Formula 1 analysis](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/analysis-what-is-going-on-with-bottas-and-cadillac.1KzNAtutw1HxVernUWD5TG), Lawrence Barretto writes that the team has yet to commit to the option in Bottas's contract that would keep him for 2027. He calls Bottas the favorite to stay, with Herta "firmly in contention."

[GPblog reports](https://www.gpblog.com/en/news/general-motors-warms-to-herta-as-cadillac-holds-off-on-bottas-contract-option), citing several sources, that General Motors is keen to move Herta up from his test role, while people inside the team value the continuity Bottas would bring. GPblog also says Esteban Ocon has contacted Cadillac and that Yuki Tsunoda has been linked with the seat. [BBC Sport](https://www.bbc.com/sport/formula1/articles/cke8rlrrzpqyo) notes that Cadillac made no mention of Bottas's future when it announced the Perez extension on Wednesday.

## What Perez said

Asked on Thursday whether he wants Herta as a teammate, Perez told the [FIA press conference](https://www.fia.com/news/f1-2026-mexico-city-grand-prix-thursday-press-conference-transcript) that Herta is "doing a good job" and bringing good feedback, but that the call belongs to Budkowski. "I'm also working really well with Valtteri," he said. "In Austin, he showed very strong pace, so there isn't an easy decision for the team." He added that he will not be consulted on the choice and does not want to be.

## Why it matters for US fans

A Herta promotion would put an American driver in a race seat at the American team. For now, Cadillac says the illness does not currently affect the rest of Bottas's weekend, and it has given no timeline for a 2027 decision.
