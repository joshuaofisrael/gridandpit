Oliver Bearman says there is no doubt about where he wants to race next year. "I want to continue with Haas. That's definitely my goal," he told the [FIA press conference](https://www.fia.com/news/f1-2026-mexico-city-grand-prix-thursday-press-conference-transcript) in Mexico City on Thursday.

## "Not really in my hands"

Bearman said there are "a few things in the background that we're working on" and that "we're just finalising a few things on that front." Asked when news might come, he said: "I don't know, to be honest. It's not really in my hands... If it was up to me, it would have already been announced a long time ago." He added that there are some wider matters being worked on, without saying what they are.

## A new teammate is already signed

Haas has already signed Ferrari junior Rafael Câmara for 2027 in place of Esteban Ocon, [Formula 1 reports](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/ferrari-junior-rafael-camara-promoted-to-f1-with-haas-for-2027.1RSxvnFaLu8Hd3JD2ppFsz). [RacingNews365](https://racingnews365.com/oliver-bearman-issues-firm-declaration-over-f1-future-amid-new-contract-delay) counts the second Haas seat as one of three unconfirmed drives for 2027, along with both Racing Bulls cars.

## Back at the scene of his best result

Mexico City is where Bearman took fourth place last year, still his best finish. He said it was his best result but not his best performance, and that the altitude helped him hold position because cars struggle to follow each other here. He admitted Haas is not quite as competitive this year, but said the car is "quite competitive in low speed" corners, so it looks to be in a better window for a weekend like this.

## Why the constructors' fight matters

Bearman said Haas is fighting for seventh in the constructors' championship, and he cares more about that than his own position. "The delta in prize money between P7 and P8 is significant. It will affect how fast the car is next year and in the future," he said.
