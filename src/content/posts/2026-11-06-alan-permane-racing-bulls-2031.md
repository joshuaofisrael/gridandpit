Visa Cash App Racing Bulls said on 6 November 2026 that Alan Permane will remain team principal and Co CEO Sporting through 2031.

The team's [X post](https://x.com/visacashapprb/status/2118629759127376299) put it simply: Alan extends as Team Principal and Co CEO Sporting until 2031. [Formula 1's news site](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/alan-permane-signs-deal-to-stay-as-racing-bulls-team-principal-through-to-2031.cuuzgpmUrpg3TLeF14TRy) confirmed a long term contract extension through the end of 2031, matching the team's own release.

## Path to the job

Permane joined as racing director in January 2024 and became team principal in July 2025 after Laurent Mekies left for Red Bull Racing. Under the VCARB name the squad took a first podium at the 2025 Dutch Grand Prix and finished sixth in that year's constructors' championship, Formula 1 noted, matching a previous best. The same piece says the team has kept scoring points in 2026 and is still in the fight for fifth.

Permane said he is proud to continue with VCARB and pointed to the people, culture, and shared sense of direction. Oliver Mintzlaff, Red Bull's CEO of Corporate Projects and Investments, said the deal gives the team stability and continuity, and praised Permane's work alongside Co CEO Commercial Peter Bayer.

## Why it matters for Faenza

A deal that runs to 2031 locks the sporting lead through several car cycles. For a Red Bull sister team that has climbed the midfield table, keeping the same principal removes one source of winter speculation and keeps the Bayer and Permane leadership pair intact for the next phase of the project.
