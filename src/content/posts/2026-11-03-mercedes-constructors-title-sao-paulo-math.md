One week after Kimi Antonelli became the youngest champion in Formula 1 history, Mercedes has a chance to finish the job. The team leads the constructors' championship by 126 points going into the Sao Paulo Grand Prix on Sunday, November 8, and it can clinch the teams' title at Interlagos if it outscores Ferrari by at least three points, [Motorsport.com reports](https://www.motorsport.com/f1/news/how-mercedes-can-win-the-2026-f1-constructors-championship-at-the-brazilian-gp/10871090/).

Our [Mexico City result story](/articles/2026-11-02-mexico-city-gp-results-verstappen-wins-antonelli-champion/) covers how Antonelli sealed the drivers' crown. This piece is about the other title, and the arithmetic behind it.

## Where the table stands

After Mexico City, Mercedes has 630 points and Ferrari 504, with Red Bull Racing third on 382 and McLaren fourth on 365, according to the [official team standings](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2026/team) and [Crash.net](https://www.crash.net/f1/results/1109260/1/new-f1-championship-standings-after-mexico-grand-prix).

| Pos | Team | Points | Gap to Mercedes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Mercedes | 630 | n/a |
| 2 | Ferrari | 504 | 126 |
| 3 | Red Bull Racing | 382 | 248 |
| 4 | McLaren | 365 | 265 |

Only Ferrari can still catch Mercedes. Red Bull and McLaren are already further behind than the maximum points left in the season.

## Why the magic number is three

Four Grands Prix remain: Sao Paulo, Las Vegas, Qatar and Abu Dhabi, per [Motorsport.com](https://www.motorsport.com/f1/news/how-mercedes-can-win-the-2026-f1-constructors-championship-at-the-brazilian-gp/10871090/). None of them has a Sprint, and there has been no bonus point for fastest lap since 2025, as our [points system explainer](/articles/2026-11-03-f1-points-system-explained/) sets out.

That makes the ceiling easy to work out. The most a team can score in one Grand Prix is a one-two: 25 points for the win plus 18 for second, so 43. Once Sao Paulo is done, three races remain, worth at most 3 x 43 = **129 points** to any team.

So the question for Sunday is simple. If Mercedes leaves Interlagos at least 129 points clear of Ferrari, Ferrari cannot pass it. Mercedes starts the weekend 126 clear, so it needs to beat Ferrari by **three points or more** in Sao Paulo, [Motorsport.com](https://www.motorsport.com/f1/news/how-mercedes-can-win-the-2026-f1-constructors-championship-at-the-brazilian-gp/10871090/) and [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/f1/news/13607118/how-mercedes-can-win-2026-f1-constructors-championship-at-sao-paulo-grand-prix-this-sunday-with-kimi-antonelli-and-george-russell) both say.

## Results that settle it on Sunday

Motorsport.com lists three Mercedes results that guarantee the title regardless of what Ferrari does:

- **A one-two** (25 + 18 = 43 points). Ferrari can score at most 15 + 12 = 27 from third and fourth, a swing of 16.
- **First and third** (25 + 15 = 40). Ferrari's best is 18 + 12 = 30, a swing of 10.
- **First and fourth** (25 + 12 = 37). Ferrari's best is 18 + 15 = 33, a swing of 4.

Plenty of other combinations work too. What matters is the gap between the two teams' combined scores. For example, if Antonelli and George Russell finish second and fifth (18 + 10 = 28 points), Mercedes clinches as long as Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton score 25 points or fewer between them.

## How Ferrari keeps it alive

Ferrari needs to outscore Mercedes, or at least stop it pulling three or more points further clear. Its form gives it a real chance. Hamilton finished second in Mexico City and Leclerc fourth, a 30 point weekend that beat Mercedes' 23 point haul from Antonelli's third and Russell's sixth, per the [official results we reported](/articles/2026-11-02-mexico-city-gp-results-verstappen-wins-antonelli-champion/). Another weekend like that in Brazil would push the decision on to Las Vegas.

Even then, the odds stay heavily with Mercedes. To win the title, Ferrari would have to outscore Mercedes by more than 126 points over four races, about 32 points a weekend. That is roughly the gap between a Ferrari one-two and a Mercedes weekend with no points at all, repeated four times.

## The fight behind

There is still plenty to race for further down the order. Verstappen's win in Mexico moved him to second in the drivers' standings on 261 points, two ahead of Hamilton on 259, with Russell on 250 and Leclerc on 245, per [Motorsport.com](https://www.motorsport.com/f1/news/how-mercedes-can-win-the-2026-f1-constructors-championship-at-the-brazilian-gp/10871090/). Our [fight for second explainer](/articles/2026-10-28-f1-fight-for-second-russell-hamilton-verstappen-leclerc/) has the background. Among the teams, McLaren is 17 points behind Red Bull Racing for third.

## What to watch

Interlagos is a short lap with a long uphill run to the line, and rain is common in Sao Paulo, which has shuffled results in the past. A wet race or an early Safety Car could easily swing more than three points either way. If Mercedes gets both cars home ahead of both Ferraris, though, the 2026 season will have both of its titles settled with three races to spare.
