Carlos Sainz made his 250th Formula 1 start one to remember. [Formula 1 posted on X](https://x.com/F1/status/2124441255669059864) after the Las Vegas Grand Prix that the Williams driver went from P20 to P8, and later [shared the moment](https://x.com/F1/status/2124466170145526268) he pipped George Russell on the line.

## From the back of the grid

According to [Sainz's own race report](https://www.carlossainz.es/en/carlos-sainz-race-las-vegas-gp-f1-2026), he started 20th after a new turbo was fitted to his car. He made what he called his best start of the season and was 12th by the end of the opening lap.

He was tenth after pitting under the Safety Car. On the final run to the flag he passed Russell's Mercedes right at the finish line, and he then moved up to eighth after a penalty for Charles Leclerc.

It was his first points finish since Baku and, his report says, a season-high result.

## What Sainz said

"I'm very happy and proud of our performance today! P8 might not seem much, but we delivered on track and executed a flawless race," Sainz said. "I did the best start of the season and we took every chance that was available to us, making sure to save my battery when it mattered, which helped me overtake George over the line for P8."

He added that it was "consistent qualifying laps for the whole race" and that he was glad to "bring home some points for the team after a long season". The [Williams race report](https://www.williamsf1.com/articles/f2271f9d-3d1f-4665-9119-07454914b5c6/report-las-vegas-grand-prix-2026) called it "a Las Vegas epic".

## Why it matters

Williams have had a hard year, with 16 points in the constructors' standings after Las Vegas. Four points from eighth place is a meaningful boost for a team near the bottom of the table, where every point can change a final position.

The battery comment is worth noting too. Sainz says saving energy at the right moment is what let him pass Russell at the end, a reminder of how much energy management shapes racing under the 2026 rules.

## Next up

Sainz and Williams head to the Qatar Grand Prix at Lusail on 27-29 November.
