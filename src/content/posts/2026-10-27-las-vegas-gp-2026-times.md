The 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix starts on Saturday, November 21 at 8 PM Pacific, which is 9 PM Mountain, 10 PM Central and 11 PM Eastern. The [official timetable](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/formula-1-heineken-las-vegas-grand-prix-2026.1qmWzcBwKK5hkTbYbhVC22) lists the race at 20:00 local time over 50 laps of the 6.201 kilometer Las Vegas Strip Circuit, with Las Vegas eight hours behind UTC.

Daylight saving time will already have ended by race week, so every session below is on standard time.

## Every session in US time zones

| Session | Day (PT) | ET | CT | MT | PT |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Practice 1 | Thu, Nov 19 | 7:30 PM | 6:30 PM | 5:30 PM | 4:30 PM |
| Practice 2 | Thu, Nov 19 | 11:00 PM | 10:00 PM | 9:00 PM | 8:00 PM |
| Practice 3 | Fri, Nov 20 | 7:30 PM | 6:30 PM | 5:30 PM | 4:30 PM |
| Qualifying | Fri, Nov 20 | 11:00 PM | 10:00 PM | 9:00 PM | 8:00 PM |
| Race | Sat, Nov 21 | 11:00 PM | 10:00 PM | 9:00 PM | 8:00 PM |

All sessions fall on the same calendar day in every continental US time zone. Grid and Pit converted the times from the official local schedule and UTC offset. The [event's own schedule page](https://www.f1lasvegasgp.com/schedule/) lists the full weekend program, including support races.

Las Vegas is round 21 of the 2026 season, after Mexico City and Brazil.
