Max Verstappen and Sergio Perez will start the Sao Paulo Grand Prix from the last row after power unit changes, with Verstappen 21st and Perez 22nd in the 22-car field, [Sky Sports reported](https://www.skysports.com/f1/news/13609202/sao-paulo-gp-grid-penalties-lewis-hamilton-max-verstappen-charles-leclerc-to-drop-down-interlagos-grid). Lewis Hamilton, also sent to the back, lines up just ahead of them in 20th.

## Why both drivers drop to the back

Red Bull went over the season allowance for the internal combustion engine, turbocharger and exhaust on Verstappen's car, [Formula 1 said](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/hamilton-leclerc-and-verstappen-set-for-sao-paulo-grand-prix-penalties-after-power-unit-changes.7B5CuFFCN1F6Txc8YwcEhd), giving him 30 places of penalties. Cadillac fitted Perez with a new Ferrari engine, turbocharger and MGU-K, which also means a back-of-the-grid start. Under the rules, any driver with more than 15 places of penalties starts at the back regardless of qualifying, and the order among them follows qualifying.

Red Bull team principal Laurent Mekies told Sky Sports before final practice: "He's done a few miracles in the past starting from the back here and we're sure he will do a few more this year." Verstappen won from 17th on the grid at Interlagos in 2024 and came from the pit lane to finish third in 2025.

## Provisional starting grid

Five drivers took penalties: George Russell (five places), Charles Leclerc (10 places), Hamilton, Verstappen and Perez. ## What is at stake

Sky Sports said Verstappen, Hamilton, Russell and Leclerc are covered by 16 points in the fight for second in the drivers' standings with four races to go, and that Mercedes can clinch the constructors' title on Sunday if they outscore their rivals by three points or more. Rain is forecast. The race starts at 14:00 local time (17:00 GMT).
