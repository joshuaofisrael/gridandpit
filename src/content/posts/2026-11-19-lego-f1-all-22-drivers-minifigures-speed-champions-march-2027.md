The whole F1 grid has been shrunk to brick size. "Our stars like you've never seen them before… introducing the F1 grid as LEGO minifigures," [F1 posted on X](https://x.com/F1/status/2123212314740760815) on Wednesday night from Las Vegas, adding that the figures are "coming as part of Speed Champions sets in March 2027".

## What is in the range

According to [F1's announcement](https://corp.formula1.com/formula-1-and-the-lego-group-take-partnership-to-the-next-level-as-first-ever-full-grid-f1-driver-lego-minifigures-revealed/), the new LEGO Formula 1 Speed Champions collection features every F1 driver as a minifigure for the first time, alongside brick-built versions of all 11 teams' 2026 regulation cars. That means 22 drivers across 11 sets.

All products go on sale on 1 March 2027 at LEGO.com and LEGO Stores worldwide, with pre-orders starting today in select regions. F1 did not list prices in its announcement.

## A LEGO race in Las Vegas

The reveal came at the Las Vegas Grand Prix, where LEGO is staging its first LEGO Race on Saturday 21 November before the Grand Prix. F1 says the drivers will "hand over the keys to a very special line up" who will race the brick-built F1 minicars, each made from more than 28,000 bricks and capable of up to 25 km/h. Who is driving has not been announced.

Emily Prazer, F1's chief commercial officer, said: "Seeing all 22 drivers recreated as LEGO minifigures is a brilliant example of what makes this partnership so much fun for fans." Julia Goldin, the LEGO Group's chief product and marketing officer, said it is "just the beginning", with more products and experiences planned for 2027.
