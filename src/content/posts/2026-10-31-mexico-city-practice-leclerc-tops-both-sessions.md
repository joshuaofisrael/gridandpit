Ferrari's Charles Leclerc was the fastest driver in both practice sessions on Friday at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez. His best lap of 1:17.827 in second practice put him 0.103 seconds ahead of McLaren's Lando Norris, with Mercedes driver George Russell just 0.003 seconds further back, according to the [FIA's session report](https://www.fia.com/news/f1-leclerc-continues-set-pace-mexico-ahead-norris-and-russell).

Championship leader Kimi Antonelli was fourth, 0.217 seconds off the pace, and Max Verstappen was fifth for Red Bull. Antonelli can clinch the title on Sunday, and our guide to [how he can do it in Mexico City](/articles/2026-10-27-antonelli-title-mexico-scenarios/) explains the numbers.

## FP2 top ten

The [Formula 1 classification](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/fp2-leclerc-tops-eventful-second-practice-from-norris-and-russell-at-mexico-city-grand-prix.5iyjNG9TZGc6DfLTacb05D), matched by [F1i's results table](https://f1i.com/news/576521-mexico-city-grand-prix-free-practice-2-results.html), had this order at the top:

1. Charles Leclerc, Ferrari, 1:17.827
2. Lando Norris, McLaren, 0.103 seconds back
3. George Russell, Mercedes, 0.106 back
4. Kimi Antonelli, Mercedes, 0.217 back
5. Max Verstappen, Red Bull, 0.311 back
6. Isack Hadjar, Red Bull, 0.410 back
7. Arvid Lindblad, Racing Bulls, 0.562 back
8. Pierre Gasly, Alpine, 0.580 back
9. Liam Lawson, Racing Bulls, 0.628 back
10. Oscar Piastri, McLaren, 0.642 back

Cadillac's Sergio Pérez was 18th and Valtteri Bottas 21st. Audi's Gabriel Bortoleto was last after a problem with his car kept him in the garage for about three quarters of the session, according to the team notes published by [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/what-the-teams-said-friday-in-mexico-city-2026.6smOV3lTU4s76z0vrnd1RJ).

## Leclerc also led the first hour

In first practice, when 10 of the 11 teams put young drivers in one car, Leclerc set a 1:18.128 on soft tires to finish 0.659 seconds clear of Antonelli, the [FIA reports](https://www.fia.com/news/f1-leclerc-tops-fp1-mexico-city-f1-rookies-get-chance-shine). Mercedes reserve Frederik Vesti was third in Russell's car. We look at how the rookies, including Americans Jak Crawford and Colton Herta, got on in [our FP1 rookie report](/articles/2026-10-31-mexico-city-fp1-rookies-crawford-herta-tsolov-slater/).

## Hamilton stops before his long run

Lewis Hamilton, fourth in FP1, ended second practice in 11th after stopping on track with a power unit problem before his long run. "I was feeling really good in the car," he told the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/sport/formula1/articles/cqde8p6j9ye7o). "It's just my luck." In Ferrari's notes published by [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/what-the-teams-said-friday-in-mexico-city-2026.6smOV3lTU4s76z0vrnd1RJ), he said the team would investigate the issue overnight. Hamilton sits close to Russell in the fight for second, as we set out in [the battle behind Antonelli](/articles/2026-10-28-f1-fight-for-second-russell-hamilton-verstappen-leclerc/).

## Red flag and penalties

The session was stopped to clear debris after Verstappen and Lawson made contact in the stadium section. The stewards later gave each driver a one place grid drop for Sunday, which we cover in [Verstappen and Lawson each drop one grid place](/articles/2026-10-31-verstappen-lawson-grid-penalties-mexico-practice/). Bottas also faces a start from the back after taking new power unit parts, explained in [our Cadillac penalty story](/articles/2026-10-31-bottas-back-of-grid-mexico-power-unit-penalty/).

## What the drivers expect

Russell told the BBC that "Ferrari are looking strongest at the moment" but that the top of qualifying "can go any way." McLaren's Randy Singh said in the team's notes that the top four teams were separated by very small gaps. Leclerc was more cautious, saying in Ferrari's notes that the team still needed to work on some limiting factors on a slippery surface.
