Max Verstappen won the Mexico City Grand Prix on Sunday, finishing 0.799 seconds ahead of Lewis Hamilton, according to the [official race result](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2026/races/1298/mexico/race-result). Third place was enough for Kimi Antonelli to clinch the 2026 drivers' title, [BBC Sport reported](https://www.bbc.com/sport/formula1/articles/cv62y1e6wly6o), so there is a winner of the race and a separate winner of the championship on the same afternoon at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez.

It was Verstappen's third win of the season and his third in the last four Grands Prix, [Formula 1's own race report says](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/antonelli-crowned-youngest-ever-f1-champion-as-verstappen-wins-eventful-mexico-city-grand-prix.xpMMeef0KmRURs9IDs3LH), a run that [Autosport](https://www.autosport.com/f1/news/f1-mexico-gp-kimi-antonelli-clinches-world-title-as-max-verstappen-holds-off-lewis-hamilton-for-vic/10870724/) also notes. Antonelli finished third, 4.006 seconds back, enough to seal the title with four rounds still to run.

## How Sunday played out

The race was stopped under red flag after Cadillac's Valtteri Bottas crashed, and the field restarted from a standing start, the [official Formula 1 report says](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/antonelli-crowned-youngest-ever-f1-champion-as-verstappen-wins-eventful-mexico-city-grand-prix.xpMMeef0KmRURs9IDs3LH). A late Safety Car followed a collision between Arvid Lindblad and Oscar Piastri, which bunched the field again before the restart, [BBC Sport reported](https://www.bbc.com/sport/formula1/articles/cv62y1e6wly6o).

Ferrari's Charles Leclerc led early after passing Hamilton on the opening lap, but he served a five second penalty during his pit stop, which handed the lead to Verstappen, [Formula 1's report says](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/antonelli-crowned-youngest-ever-f1-champion-as-verstappen-wins-eventful-mexico-city-grand-prix.xpMMeef0KmRURs9IDs3LH). [Autosport](https://www.autosport.com/f1/news/how-ferraris-loss-became-verstappens-gain-at-f1s-mexico-gp/10870831/) called it a day when Ferrari threw away its own chance of victory while Verstappen made the most of the chaos around him.

Pirelli's post race report says Verstappen ran a two stop strategy, Medium tires to a new set of Softs on lap 27, then another Soft after the red flag, and that he held off Hamilton's late pressure to the finish, according to [Pirelli](https://press.pirelli.com/the-champions-podium-verstappen-wins-in-mexico-as-kimi-secures-the-title/). Antonelli started on Softs, switched to Mediums on lap 43 and fitted a used set of Softs at the restart, a strategy [Pirelli said](https://press.pirelli.com/the-champions-podium-verstappen-wins-in-mexico-as-kimi-secures-the-title/) was enough to secure third place and the title.

## Antonelli clinches the title

Antonelli went into the race needing only ninth place to become champion, regardless of what Hamilton or his Mercedes teammate George Russell did, [Formula 1's report says](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/antonelli-crowned-youngest-ever-f1-champion-as-verstappen-wins-eventful-mexico-city-grand-prix.xpMMeef0KmRURs9IDs3LH). He started fourth and finished third, enough to seal the crown with four rounds to spare, according to both [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/antonelli-crowned-youngest-ever-f1-champion-as-verstappen-wins-eventful-mexico-city-grand-prix.xpMMeef0KmRURs9IDs3LH) and the [AP wire report](https://apnews.com/article/antonelli-formula-1-mexico-city-grand-prix-370087f112c6c26db286b98d6f23c96a).

At 20 years old, Antonelli is the youngest champion in Formula 1 history, beating the record Sebastian Vettel set in 2010, and the first Italian champion since Alberto Ascari in 1953, both [BBC Sport](https://www.bbc.com/sport/formula1/articles/cv62y1e6wly6o) and [Autosport](https://www.autosport.com/f1/news/f1-mexico-gp-kimi-antonelli-clinches-world-title-as-max-verstappen-holds-off-lewis-hamilton-for-vic/10870724/) report. "Thank you so much. We did it. We did it," Antonelli said on his slowing down lap, [BBC Sport reported](https://www.bbc.com/sport/formula1/articles/cv62y1e6wly6o), a radio message also captured in the [FIA's post race transcript](https://www.fia.com/news/f1-2026-mexico-city-grand-prix-post-race-world-champions-press-conference-transcript).

## Standings after Mexico City
