Mercedes won the 2026 Formula 1 constructors' championship at the Sao Paulo Grand Prix on Sunday. George Russell finished fourth and Kimi Antonelli fifth behind race winner Lando Norris, [Formula 1 reported](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/sao-paulo-lowdown-all-the-key-moments-as-mercedes-secures-constructors-title-and-norris-wins-in-the-rain.2VjsvSp3rejjziq7VbVVOr). It is the team's first constructors' title since 2021 and comes one weekend after Antonelli won his first drivers' championship.

## Russell's penalty and pit-exit spin

Mercedes came to Interlagos all but certain of the title. In Saturday's wet qualifying, Russell was second and Antonelli third. A five-place grid penalty for an internal combustion engine (ICE) change dropped Russell to seventh on the grid.

Russell climbed to fourth before the switch to slick tires, then spun on the damp pit exit after his stop and brushed the wall while turning his car around. He spent the middle of the race around the edge of the top 10 before working his way back up.

## Antonelli's two spins

Antonelli spun on the grass at Turn 12 on his reconnaissance lap before the start and returned to the garage for checks. When the lights went out, he took the lead from second on the grid.

After the pit stops, he told the team "something is weird with the car" and suggested a suspension problem. He spun at Turn 1 at the start of Lap 20, which gave the lead to Norris, and later ran wide at the same corner and lost places to both Red Bulls.

When rain returned late in the race, both Mercedes pitted for intermediates. Russell, who had switched to intermediates a lap earlier, closed on his teammate, and Mercedes told Antonelli to let him through for fourth.

## Penalty does not change the result

After the race, the stewards gave Antonelli a five-second penalty for an unsafe release at his final stop, where he pulled into the path of an Alpine. He stayed fifth in the [official classification](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2026/races/1299/brazil/race-result), so the title result was unaffected. Russell scored 12 points and Antonelli 10.

With the drivers' and constructors' titles both settled, the remaining rounds start with Las Vegas on November 20-22.
