Las Vegas finally got some running, and George Russell timed it best. "Fastest in FP2: George, Pierre, Charles," [F1 posted on X](XF1) after Thursday night's session on the Strip.

## A last-gasp lap

According to the [official F1 report](FP2), Russell set a 1m 47.272s on his final lap as the chequered flag came out, 0.071s clear of Pierre Gasly's Alpine. Charles Leclerc was third, just over three tenths back, ahead of Isack Hadjar and Oscar Piastri.

The whole hour was wet. FP1 had produced no competitive lap times at all, so teams were desperate for mileage. Piastri set the first time on full wets, then Lando Norris was first to try intermediates after about 15 minutes and topped the times for a while. Leclerc became the first under 1m 50s, saying over the radio that conditions were "probably close to slick tyres".

## Red flag and a blocked lap

With just over ten minutes left, the session was stopped. "We have a red flag, and the drivers return to the pit lane. Looks like there's some debris on track," [F1 posted](https://x.com/F1/status/2123640380428050519). The F1 report says pieces of plastic had blown onto the circuit at the final corner. Running resumed with about six minutes left, everyone still on intermediates.

Hadjar looked set to go quickest before he was held up by Ollie Bearman's Haas in the final corners. Lewis Hamilton, Norris, Kimi Antonelli, Franco Colapinto and Fernando Alonso completed the top ten.

## Verstappen's quiet night

Max Verstappen was only 13th. The F1 report says he set a time only in the closing minutes and reported problems with his car. Alonso and Norris both ran wide during the session but avoided damage.
