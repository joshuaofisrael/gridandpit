Lando Norris won the 2026 Sao Paulo Grand Prix at Interlagos on Sunday, finishing 14.602 seconds ahead of Max Verstappen in a race that started wet, dried out and then caught a late shower. Isack Hadjar was third, according to the [official race classification](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2026/races/1299/brazil/race-result). It was Norris's third win of the season, and he converted pole position over 71 laps in 1:35:23.504.

Sergio Perez (Cadillac), Carlos Sainz (Williams) and Rafael Camara (Haas, on his F1 debut) completed the classified finishers in 18th to 20th. Lance Stroll retired after 30 laps, and Valtteri Bottas did not start after an issue with his Cadillac.

## Post-race penalties

The stewards gave Antonelli and Bearman five-second penalties for unsafe releases, and Leclerc five seconds for causing a collision with Lawson. Leclerc had passed Lawson on the penultimate lap, so his penalty swapped the two back and gave Lawson seventh. Antonelli kept fifth.

## How the race unfolded

According to the [F1 race report](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/norris-beats-verstappen-in-wet-sao-paulo-gp-thriller-as-mercedes-win-constructors-championship.7JN7MXLZf5lk5l0C3gfKGI), Antonelli jumped polesitter Norris at the start, and Lawson, who started third, went around the outside to take second. Norris pitted for slicks at the end of Lap 16, a lap before Antonelli. The Mercedes driver then reported that "something is weird with the car" and spun at Turn 1 at the start of Lap 20, handing Norris the lead.

Norris made his second stop at the end of Lap 49 and moved to intermediates when rain returned in the closing laps.

Verstappen started 21st after power-unit penalties. He reached 13th on the opening lap and eighth by Lap 5. He then took second from teammate Hadjar on Lap 38 after Hadjar ran wide at Turn 1. Hamilton, on his 400th F1 start, gained 11 places on Lap 1 from 20th on the grid before tire wear, a track-limits investigation and a late spin left him ninth.

"It was a very tough race, very, very tough," Norris said. "The main thing today was I just kept it cool and I didn't make any mistakes."

The championship moves on to the Las Vegas Grand Prix on November 20-22.
