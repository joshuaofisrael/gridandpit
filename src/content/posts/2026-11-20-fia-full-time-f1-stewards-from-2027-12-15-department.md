One of the paddock's oldest complaints is getting an answer. "From 2027, the FIA will begin introducing full-time stewards to the FIA Formula One World Championship, strengthening consistency, expertise and continuity in race decision-making," [the FIA posted on X](https://x.com/fia/status/2123506603806617606) on Thursday.

## What changes

According to the [FIA's announcement](https://www.fia.com/news/fia-introduce-full-time-stewards-fia-formula-one-world-championship-2027), the Federation will recruit a small number of full-time stewards during the 2027 season and expand the group from 2028. The aim is a department of 12 to 15 full-time stewards over the coming years, which the X post puts at "the next five years".

The current system will not disappear overnight. Existing stewards will work in a mixed roster of full-time and part-time officials "for the foreseeable future", and each event will still have a former driver on the panel. The FIA says it will now open recruitment for experienced international stewards.

## Why it matters

Drivers and teams have long complained that penalties vary from one weekend to the next because panels change. The FIA says full-time stewards will bring "greater consistency in decision-making" and give it capacity between races to spot trends and areas where the rules need clarifying. The department could later support other FIA single-seater series.

"This latest major investment in FIA officiating reflects our dedication to delivering the highest standards of decision-making, fairness and integrity in motorsport," FIA President Mohammed Ben Sulayem said. The move follows last month's opening of a new Remote Operations Centre at Biggin Hill in the UK.

The reaction in Las Vegas was positive. [Motorsport.com](MSV8) reports that McLaren CEO Zak Brown praised the move in Thursday's team principals' press conference.

## What we don't know

The FIA has not said how many stewards will be hired in 2027, at which races they will start, or what the programme costs. Rule changes are also under discussion elsewhere, including the [straight-line energy clipping fix](/articles/2026-11-19-fia-considers-2027-change-high-speed-deployment-clipping-hamilton/) we covered on Thursday.
