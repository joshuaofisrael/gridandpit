Alpine's best night in a while came under the Las Vegas lights. [Formula 1 posted on X](https://x.com/F1/status/2124471706378998157) celebrating double points for the team after both drivers finished in the top six.

## The result

According to [Autosport's results](https://www.autosport.com/f1/results/2026/las-vegas-gp-661701/), Pierre Gasly finished fifth for 10 points, and Franco Colapinto was sixth for eight more.

Max Verstappen won the race ahead of Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri, with Kimi Antonelli fourth. That made Gasly the first driver home behind Verstappen, the two McLarens and Antonelli's Mercedes.

## Where it leaves Alpine

The [official team standings](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2026/team) now show Alpine on 87 points in sixth place. Racing Bulls are fifth on 114, so the gap above is 27 points. Below, Haas are seventh on 31, so sixth place looks secure with two races left.

## Why it matters

A double top-six finish is the kind of result midfield teams need to move up the table. Points scored this late in the season also help a team's final position, which decides its share of prize money.

For Colapinto, sixth is a strong result to finish the season on. For Gasly, it is another sign that he can bring the car home in the points when the chances come.

## What to watch

The next round is the Qatar Grand Prix at Lusail on 27-29 November. Alpine would need another big weekend and a quiet one from Racing Bulls to close the 27-point gap. The more realistic target is to keep scoring with both cars and lock in sixth.
