George Russell has the best car on the grid and is still second in the drivers' championship, but he admitted on Thursday in Mexico City that 2026 has been the most up and down season of his career. "I've never had a season like this in my career where the performance has fluctuated so much," he said in the [FIA press conference](https://www.fia.com/news/f1-2026-mexico-city-grand-prix-thursday-press-conference-transcript).

## A narrow window

Mercedes boss Toto Wolff said after Austin that the team's upgrade package works in a very narrow performance window. Russell agreed. He said the package was first fitted in Malaysia, ran on Kimi Antonelli's car in Singapore, and was back on his car in Austin, where he "struggled a lot" while Antonelli won. "We are seeing some things in the characteristics that just show that it performed in a very narrow window. So we need to try and widen that," Russell said.

He listed the swings himself. He was quickest in every session in Barcelona, took pole in Austria, then qualified fifth or sixth a few races later. In Baku he was on pole by eight tenths and won, and last weekend he finished eighth. "Now this season, we have the best car, and I'm not able to achieve these results," he said.

## Why his style is being punished

Asked what makes the car hard to drive, Russell gave two reasons. He has always focused on the middle and exit of corners and gets on the throttle earlier than most, and with the 2026 power units he said that "means I'm spending more energy," which is not always the most efficient way to drive. He also pointed to very high rear tire pressures, saying Pirelli has taken a few PSI of margin on the rear axle. "These two factors have really hampered my natural driving style," he said.

## Antonelli and the title

Russell was candid about his teammate, who leads the championship on 365 points. "The truth is he's been at a higher level than me this year," he said, adding that the two have kept a good relationship because they have rarely been in close fights on track.

He also rejected the idea of planning a head to head campaign against Antonelli for 2027. "We're not boxers where you're in a ring fighting directly against somebody else. We're kind of fighting against ourselves, almost like a golfer," he said.

## The fight for second

Only nine points cover second to fifth. Russell has 242, Lewis Hamilton 241, Max Verstappen 236 and Charles Leclerc 233, according to the [official standings](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2026/drivers). Russell said finishing second matters less to him than ending the year with momentum: "As long as it's not P1, in this sport it's kind of that or nothing, really." Our [fight for second explainer](/articles/2026-10-28-f1-fight-for-second-russell-hamilton-verstappen-leclerc/) breaks down the gaps.

He was also clear that one hard season will not define him. "I remind myself I'm 28 years old," he said, pointing to Hamilton at 41 and Fernando Alonso at 44. "I've still got a good 12 to 15 years left in me."
