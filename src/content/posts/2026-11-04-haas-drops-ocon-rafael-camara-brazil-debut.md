Haas has ended Esteban Ocon’s 2026 racing duties and will hand Brazilian prospect Rafael Câmara his Formula 1 debut at this weekend’s São Paulo Grand Prix, bringing forward a driver change originally scheduled for next season.

Câmara will partner Oliver Bearman at Interlagos on November 6–8. Haas had already selected Câmara to replace Ocon for 2027, but the French driver’s removal during the Mexico City Grand Prix weekend has accelerated that transition. The team has confirmed that Ocon will not return to its race seat this year, according to [RacingNews365](https://racingnews365.com/haas-announce-esteban-ocon-replacement-for-sao-paulo-f1-grand-prix) and [Formula 1’s official report](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/f2-racer-camara-to-make-f1-debut-at-sao-paulo-grand-prix-as-haas-confirm-ocon-will-not-return.12Qa0tpaMZ9kgnEuGJBKFp).

## A Sudden End to Ocon’s Season

Ocon was stood down on the Saturday of the Mexico City Grand Prix weekend. Haas said the decision was not related to illness but did not publicly detail the internal discussions behind it. RacingNews365 reported that a heated disagreement after Friday practice was believed to have involved team principal Ayao Komatsu and race engineer Laura Mueller over the second practice session’s running plan.

Ocon publicly rejected allegations that physical violence had occurred. The distinction matters: reports of a disagreement should not be treated as proof of a physical confrontation. Haas has likewise declined to disclose what was said behind closed doors.

In its [official driver update](https://www.haasf1team.com/news/team-statement-driver-update-0), Haas confirmed that Ocon would not race for the team again in 2026. Komatsu said the decision was made in what the team considered its best interests and described the conversations with Ocon as an internal matter.

The decision ends Ocon’s Haas racing campaign earlier than expected. His departure for 2027 had already been announced, but until the events in Mexico, the change was due to take effect after the current season.

## Why Câmara Gets the Seat Now

Reserve driver Ryo Hirakawa replaced Ocon in Mexico City and finished tenth, earning a point on his Formula 1 race debut. However, Hirakawa is committed to Toyota’s World Endurance Championship programme this weekend, with its season finale at Monza clashing with the São Paulo race.

That left Haas weighing alternatives, including reserve Jack Doohan. [RacingNews365 reported](https://racingnews365.com/haas-announce-esteban-ocon-replacement-for-sao-paulo-f1-grand-prix) that the team secured Câmara’s early release from Ferrari and that he becomes Haas’s fourth race driver of 2026, the maximum permitted under the applicable rules. The outlet also reported that Haas had not confirmed its second-car plans beyond Brazil.

Câmara arrives with a substantial junior-racing résumé. A Ferrari Driver Academy member since 2021, he has won the Formula Regional European Championship and Formula 3 titles and currently leads the Formula 2 drivers’ standings. His first Haas track experience came in August, when he drove the team’s 2025 car in a previous-car test at Portimão, Portugal.

Komatsu pointed to that test in explaining the choice. The team said Câmara had impressed during the outing, giving Haas a basis for confidence before his first competitive F1 weekend.

## A Home Debut With Immediate Stakes

For Câmara, the timing is remarkable: rather than waiting until 2027, he will enter Formula 1 at his home Grand Prix, alongside Bearman, in a car he has not yet raced. The opportunity is both an early start to his Haas career and a demanding introduction to the championship.

The confirmed arrangement covers Câmara’s appearance at São Paulo; Haas has not announced a complete race-by-race lineup for the remaining rounds. What is settled is that Ocon’s 2026 spell is over and that Brazil will see the beginning of Câmara’s Formula 1 racing career sooner than originally planned.
