Max Verstappen and Liam Lawson will each start Sunday's Mexico City Grand Prix one place lower than where they qualify. The stewards handed both drivers a one place grid drop and one penalty point after they made contact during second practice on Friday, [Formula 1 reports](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/verstappen-and-lawson-get-grid-penalties-after-clumsy-mexico-city-practice-clash.2m2ey8Pl7g5IfQfGeepilD).

## What happened

Both drivers had sat out first practice while rookies Ayumu Iwasa and Nikola Tsolov drove their cars. Early in FP2, Verstappen passed Lawson into Turn 12. Lawson tried to get back ahead through the stadium section, and the two cars touched side by side, damaging the floor of the Red Bull and the front wing of the Racing Bulls car. A red flag was shown to clear debris. Verstappen still finished fifth and Lawson ninth.

## Why the stewards went beyond a warning

According to the stewards' decision quoted by Formula 1, Verstappen was on a preparation lap and Lawson on a cool down lap before another push lap. The stewards said both drivers behaved in a way they "considered unacceptable", noting a warning given at the team managers' meeting earlier that day, and added that "both drivers admitted the moves were unnecessary and 'clumsy'."

Because there had been similar incidents in practice this season, the stewards said they escalated the penalty from a formal warning to a grid drop. A practice incident leading to a race grid penalty is unusual, as the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/sport/formula1/articles/cqde8p6j9ye7o) notes. The decision summary published by [gp.news](https://gp.news/documents/2026-mexico-city-grand-prix-doc-33/) gives the same one place drop and one penalty point, and the original documents are on the [FIA decision page](https://www.fia.com/documents/event/Mexico%20City%20Grand%20Prix).

## What the drivers and Racing Bulls said

Speaking before the verdict, Verstappen told the BBC it was "maybe not very smart behaviour" from Lawson and that "at the end of the day it is not going to change my weekend." In Red Bull's notes published by [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/what-the-teams-said-friday-in-mexico-city-2026.6smOV3lTU4s76z0vrnd1RJ), he said the car picked up some damage from the incident.

Racing Bulls team principal Alan Permane described the clash in one word: "unnecessary." He said he applied that to both drivers and that "they can both do better."

## The seat question behind it

The BBC points out that Lawson is one of three drivers in contention for two Racing Bulls seats in 2027, alongside teammate Arvid Lindblad and Formula 2 front runner Nikola Tsolov.
