Formula 1 said Kimi Antonelli ended Friday's second practice on top at the Sao Paulo Grand Prix.

The championship [posted the FP2 classification](https://x.com/F1/status/2118797838423568791) with Antonelli first, then followed with a [report link](https://x.com/F1/status/2118800191235178669) saying he led Lando Norris and Max Verstappen. Mercedes [confirmed](https://x.com/MercedesAMGF1/status/2118796909498503191) that Antonelli was fastest and that George Russell finished Friday in fourth.

## What Friday means for Saturday

Practice order is not the grid. Teams were still gathering long-run and single-lap data on a low-grip Interlagos surface, and Mercedes later posted driver comments describing tricky balance work between FP1 and FP2. Still, ending Friday P1 gives Antonelli and Mercedes a clean headline into qualifying day in Brazil.

For the public tally, stick to the [F1 classification post](https://x.com/F1/status/2118797838423568791) and the [Mercedes confirmation](https://x.com/MercedesAMGF1/status/2118796909498503191) until official timing sheets say otherwise.
