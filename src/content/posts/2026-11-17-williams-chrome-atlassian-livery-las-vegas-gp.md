Williams will race a special chrome livery at this weekend's Las Vegas Grand Prix, [Formula 1 reports](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/williams-unveil-special-one-off-livery-for-las-vegas-grand-prix.01klQqq0PYu7wjugCtr98K). The one-off design is a collaboration with title partner Atlassian and is meant to shine under the lights of the Las Vegas Strip Circuit.

## What changes

The FW48 cars of Alex Albon and Carlos Sainz will carry the chrome look for the whole weekend, and both drivers will wear custom race suits and gloves to match, according to the F1 report. The team said the design is "the latest embodiment of Williams and Atlassian's belief that the fastest way forward is knowing where you have been".

## What the team said

Team principal James Vowles said the next step for Williams is "about evolving from where we've been", and that the team was proud to work with Atlassian to unlock its near 50-year history. Atlassian co-founder and chief executive Mike Cannon-Brookes said the partnership goes beyond the car, with the two working on AI workflows on Atlassian's Teamwork Graph to help the team make faster decisions.

## Why Vegas

Special liveries have become a regular part of the Las Vegas weekend, where the race runs late at night under artificial light. A reflective finish is designed to stand out on television under those conditions, although it changes nothing about how the car performs.
