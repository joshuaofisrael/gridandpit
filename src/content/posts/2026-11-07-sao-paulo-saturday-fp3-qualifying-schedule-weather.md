Saturday at Interlagos decides the Sao Paulo Grand Prix grid, with Free Practice 3 and qualifying scheduled under warmer and less settled weather than Friday’s running.

## Session times

[Motorsport.com](https://www.motorsport.com/f1/news/f1-brazil-gp-saturday-schedule-weather-forecast-and-how-to-watch/10872353/) lists Saturday’s local (Sao Paulo) timetable as:

- Free Practice 3 — 11:30 BRT
- Qualifying — 15:00 BRT

Converted for readers elsewhere, that is 14:30 and 18:00 GMT, or 09:30 and 13:00 US Eastern. Sunday’s race is listed for 14:00 BRT on 8 November.

## Weather risk

Motorsport.com’s Saturday forecast called for warmer conditions, roughly 26–28C for FP3 with showers possible around midday, then 27–29C for qualifying with a higher chance of showers and thunderstorms. Strong winds, with gusts potentially above 40–50 km/h, are another complication on a short lap where track temperature and grip can swing quickly.

That forecast matters because Friday already left the field closely matched at the front. [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/fp2-antonelli-tops-the-times-from-norris-in-second-practice-at-sao-paulo-gp.27XFbTG9Lji18qmtHbCJUA) had Antonelli fastest in FP2 from Norris and Verstappen, separated by only a few hundredths among the chasing group. A damp qualifying hour would amplify small tyre and timing errors.

## Extra grid wrinkle

Motorsport.com also noted that George Russell carries a five-place grid penalty into Sunday after Mercedes fitted a sixth internal combustion engine. That does not change Saturday’s qualifying classification, but it does change how teams may treat the transfer of Saturday pace into Sunday starting positions.

## How to watch

Broadcast rights vary by territory. Motorsport.com notes Apple TV as the primary US partner for 2026, Sky Sports for the UK, and F1 TV as the global subscription stream. Check local listings for FP3 and qualifying start times in your zone; the BRT clock above is the circuit-local reference.

Interlagos remains a 4.309 km, 15-corner anti-clockwise venue with the downhill Senna S and a long uphill run to the line. With unsettled weather on the schedule, FP3’s main job is less about absolute lap time than confirming wet or dry setup confidence before the 15:00 quali window.
