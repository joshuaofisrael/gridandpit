F1's plan to put drivers' heart rates on TV has its first high-profile refusal. "Max Verstappen won't take part in next year's experiment to show F1 drivers' heart rates to fans," [Autosport posted on X](https://x.com/autosport/status/2123340503689379962) on Thursday.

## What Verstappen said

Speaking at the Las Vegas media day, Verstappen was blunt, according to [Motorsport.com](https://www.motorsport.com/f1/news/max-verstappen-on-f1s-whoop-plan-its-nobodys-business-i-dont-even-know-my-heart-rate/10875480/). "I won't be involved. I don't use any device, also not for my training, I don't use anything. First of all, I don't like to be tracked and I know when I'm tired," he said.

Asked if it was about privacy, he said: "Yes, also that. People don't need to know my heart rate. I also don't want to know my heart rate! I know when it's high and I know when it's low." He added that other drivers are free to share if they want.

## Leclerc is in

Charles Leclerc, a WHOOP ambassador, said fans can expect to see his data next year. "I don't have anything to hide in particular," he said. He welcomed the fact that there is now an FIA-certified device drivers can wear in the car openly, though he said showing the data to everyone is "another matter that we'll for sure discuss".

## Russell sees a catch

George Russell sits in the middle. "As a neutral, if I was sat on the sofa, I think I'd really enjoy seeing it," he said. But he warned that resting heart rates vary a lot by age and person, so fans could draw the wrong conclusions, and said he would sympathise with a driver whose natural heart rate is 20 beats higher than a rival's.

## How the plan works

As we reported this morning, F1 has [signed a deal with WHOOP](/articles/2026-11-19-f1-whoop-partnership-driver-heart-rate-tv-2027/) to bring driver data such as heart rate to broadcasts as early as 2027. Taking part is optional, and Motorsport.com says at least a handful of drivers have already committed. The rest of the weekend is in our [Las Vegas hub](/hubs/las-vegas/).
