U.S. viewers will be able to watch the entire Las Vegas Grand Prix weekend live on Apple TV without a subscription, [Grandprix.com reported](https://www.grandprix.com/news/apple-tv-to-stream-full-las-vegas-gp-weekend-free-in-the-us.html) on 10 November. The free coverage is described as running from Thursday practice through the Saturday night race on 21 November, including all three practice sessions, Friday qualifying and the race itself.

According to that report, first practice starts at 4:30 p.m. Las Vegas time on 19 November, with the second session at 8 p.m. Friday follows the same timetable for final practice and qualifying. The race starts at 8 p.m. Las Vegas time on Saturday, 21 November, which Grandprix.com said is 11 p.m. in New York.

Viewers can use the Apple TV app on supported devices or watch in a browser on the official Las Vegas Grand Prix viewing page, the report said. Apple is in the first season of a five-year U.S. rights deal with Formula 1. Grandprix.com said the agreement makes every practice session available for free and allows Apple to offer selected races without a subscription.

Las Vegas is the first leg of Formula 1's closing triple-header, with Qatar and Abu Dhabi to follow, after F1 told Imola it would not be needed as a standby finale. The free U.S. window applies to this race weekend as described in the 10 November report; it should not be read as a promise that every remaining 2026 race will also be free. Session times can still shift if the official timetable is updated, so the Las Vegas Grand Prix's own schedule remains the reference for when cars go on track.
