Aston Martin bounced back from a bruising Mexico City Grand Prix with its most competitive weekend of the season in Brazil, even though Fernando Alonso finished just outside the points in twelfth, [Formula 1 reported](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/alonso-credits-phenomenal-car-after-strong-showing-in-sao-paulo.7d9xmDFQhJ3IRqGx0xuFHK).

## A first top ten qualifying of 2026

Alonso used his experience in the wet on Saturday to qualify inside the top ten for the first time all season. He held his place up the order through the first stint of the race and, even after dropping back, kept fighting the cars around him, including a pass on Lewis Hamilton's Ferrari on merit.

He finished ahead of both Alpines, both Williams cars and both Haas drivers. Afterwards he described the AMR26 as "phenomenal in the corners" and said it was the best the car had felt in a race.

Alonso said that in the last five laps on intermediates he was running with the two Mercedes cars, a lap down, and suggested that given how much time Aston Martin loses on the straights, the car might have been about a second a lap quicker than them through the corners. Formula 1 noted that this comment seemed partly tongue in cheek, but the AMR26 was clearly much closer to the front than it has been for most of the year.

## Reliability still hurts

The weekend was a contrast with Mexico City, where both cars retired early with power unit problems. Lance Stroll did not finish in Brazil either, retiring with an ERS issue. It was his eighth retirement in the last ten Grands Prix.

Chief Trackside Officer Mike Krack called it a day of mixed fortunes, praising Alonso's drive and saying the team should be satisfied to have run ahead of faster cars in both qualifying and the race.

## Where Aston Martin stands

Aston Martin remain tenth in the constructors' championship, five points behind Williams. Alonso last scored in Malaysia. The pace in Brazil suggests the team is moving in the right direction, but it will need both cars to finish if it is to close that gap before the season ends.
