Formula E has postponed the Jeddah E-Prix double-header that was due to open its 2026/27 season, [the championship announced](https://www.fiaformulae.com/en/news/races/formula-e-announce-2027-calendar-update-for-jeddah-and-sanya-e-prix), so Mexico City will now start the first year of the Gen4 cars.

The two Jeddah races move from 18 and 19 December 2026 to 16 and 17 April 2027, a change approved by the FIA World Motor Sport Council. Formula E said the decision was reached with its Saudi partners and the FIA "taking into account recent developments in the region and the logistical considerations involved." [Autosport noted](https://www.autosport.com/formula-e/news/mexico-to-host-formula-e-opener-after-jeddah-postponement/10874055/) the December date had long been in doubt because of conflict in the Middle East.

Formula E said the number of races and the Jeddah double-header format are unchanged. The Sanya E-Prix in China moves forward two weeks to 3 April 2027 to make room. Ticket holders will hear from official ticketing partners about the new dates, transfers and refunds, [per the release](https://www.fiaformulae.com/en/news/races/formula-e-announce-2027-calendar-update-for-jeddah-and-sanya-e-prix).

## The new start of the season

According to [Autosport](https://www.autosport.com/formula-e/news/mexico-to-host-formula-e-opener-after-jeddah-postponement/10874055/) and [Motorsport.com](https://www.motorsport.com/formula-e/news/formula-e-postpones-jeddah-e-prix-with-mexico-now-the-season-opener/10874048/), the 21-round calendar now opens like this:

| Round | Event | Date |
|---|---|---|
| Opener | Mexico City, Autodromo Hermanos Rodriguez | 16 January 2027 |
| Next | Austin | 6 February 2027 |
| Next | Miami | Two weeks after Austin |
| Next | Sao Paulo | 13 March 2027 |
| Round 7 | Sanya | 3 April 2027 |
| Then | Jeddah double-header | 16-17 April 2027 |

That puts two US races, Austin and Miami, in the first month of the season. The European run then begins in Monaco on 1-2 May, Autosport reported, followed by Berlin, a debut for Brands Hatch as the London E-Prix on 29 May, a first visit to Zandvoort on 18-19 June and Madrid a week later. Shanghai hosts the penultimate weekend on 10-11 July before a Tokyo finale two weeks after.

Pre-season testing for the new Gen4 car begins in Madrid on 16-20 November, and Porsche's Pascal Wehrlein starts as defending champion, [per Autosport](https://www.autosport.com/formula-e/news/mexico-to-host-formula-e-opener-after-jeddah-postponement/10874055/).

**Context:** Formula 1 is taking the opposite route for now. It plans to finish 2026 in Qatar and Abu Dhabi as scheduled after releasing Imola from standby, as we [reported on Wednesday](/articles/2026-11-11-f1-drops-imola-standby-qatar-abu-dhabi-proceed/).
