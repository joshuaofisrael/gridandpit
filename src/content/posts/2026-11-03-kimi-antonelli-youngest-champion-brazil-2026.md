Kimi Antonelli will arrive at the November 6–8 São Paulo Grand Prix as [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/what-time-is-the-formula-1-2026-sao-paulo-grand-prix-and-how-can-i-watch-it.6hpbTPt6gucIHBH8QztPjb)’s youngest world champion, Formula 1 confirmed in its November 3 race preview. Third place in Mexico City secured the championship for the Mercedes driver. The official preview says he has nine wins this season. Although the drivers’ title has been decided, the remaining races still matter for team standings, individual results and the shape of the 2026 season’s final chapter.

For American viewers following the series through its growing U.S. calendar, Antonelli’s title is a major storyline heading toward the Las Vegas Grand Prix later this month. Brazil comes first, and Interlagos is a demanding circuit where weather and race execution can change the competitive order. A crowned champion can still race aggressively for wins, while rivals have incentives to finish the year strongly.

## How the title was secured

The 20-year-old Italian entered Sunday's 71-lap Mexico City Grand Prix needing only ninth place to clinch the drivers' championship regardless of the results of Lewis Hamilton and Mercedes teammate George Russell. He qualified fourth and finished third, comfortably meeting that requirement. The FIA confirmed the result on November 2, and Formula 1's November 3 Brazil preview described Antonelli as arriving in São Paulo with nine wins from his title-winning campaign.

The race was far from uneventful. A heavy accident for Valtteri Bottas prompted a red flag, while a late crash involving Arvid Lindblad brought out the Safety Car. Antonelli negotiated both interruptions and remained on the podium. His title was therefore secured by an actual race result, not merely by a rival's retirement or a mathematical announcement before the start.

## Verstappen won the race, Antonelli won the year

Max Verstappen claimed the Mexico City victory after starting fifth and moving through a complicated opening sequence. The FIA reported that Ferrari's Charles Leclerc went across the grass while battling Hamilton and was subsequently given a five-second penalty for leaving the track and gaining an advantage.That result captures two different sporting narratives. Verstappen delivered the strongest race outcome in Mexico, while Antonelli had accumulated enough results across the season to become champion before the final rounds. A single Grand Prix winner is not necessarily the season's dominant points scorer, especially in a year with changing competitive performance.

## Why Brazil still matters

The São Paulo Grand Prix runs November 6–8 at Interlagos, and Mercedes can clinch the constructors' championship there. The team title depends on both Antonelli and Russell, so the new champion still has an important job. Ferrari and Red Bull also have reasons to pursue a victory after their strong Mexico City performances. The drivers' crown being decided does not make the remaining races exhibitions.

For U.S. fans, the Sunday race is scheduled for noon Eastern and 9 a.m. Pacific. Formula 1 has warned of rain possibilities during the weekend, especially on Saturday, although the forecast may change. A wet or disrupted race could alter qualifying order and strategy at a circuit known for elevation changes and overtaking opportunities into Turn 1. Antonelli arrives with the biggest individual prize secured, but the next test is how Mercedes completes its season.
