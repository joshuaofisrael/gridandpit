Formula 1 said Lewis Hamilton will start his 400th Grand Prix at the Sao Paulo weekend.

In a [post on 5 November 2026](https://x.com/F1/status/2118483750116102548), the championship's official account wrote that his career has been packed with major milestones, and that another one arrives this weekend: Lewis will start his 400th Grand Prix. The post was tagged for the Brazil GP and carried a short career montage video.

## The landmark

Four hundred starts puts Hamilton in rare company on the all time Grand Prix appearances list. Formula 1's own framing treats the Brazil race as the moment the count ticks over, not as a retrospective total after the flag. The championship did not add new championship maths in that post; the news is the start number itself.

Hamilton's weekend in Sao Paulo therefore carries a ceremonial weight even before practice times land. For Grid and Pit readers, the takeaway is simple: the most decorated driver of his era adds another round number on the Brazilian calendar, as confirmed by the series on X.
