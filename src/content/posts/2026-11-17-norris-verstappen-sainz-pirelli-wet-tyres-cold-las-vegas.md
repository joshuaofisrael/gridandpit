Lando Norris wants Pirelli to bring much better rain tyres for 2027, and he fears the current ones would struggle if it rains at this weekend's Las Vegas Grand Prix, [Autosport reports](https://www.autosport.com/f1/news/lando-norris-f1-needs-better-rain-tyres-as-wet-las-vegas-would-be-scary/10874886/).

## What Brazil showed

The Sao Paulo Grand Prix was the first fully wet qualifying session and race for the 2026 cars. Qualifying was halted for more than an hour because of standing water, and the race started and finished on intermediates. Autosport says the tyres largely worked in the right temperature window, but drivers felt this year's stiffer intermediates struggle when cold, especially at the start of a stint and on laps to the grid.

## Why Las Vegas is different

Interlagos is one of the easiest tracks for getting heat into tyres. Las Vegas is the opposite: temperatures are expected to drop well below 10C over the weekend and the circuit has no high-speed corners to load the tyres. Autosport says the FIA's official forecast was not yet out, but other forecasts put the chance of rain above 50% for first practice on Thursday evening, with drier conditions more likely after that.

## What the drivers said

Norris told Autosport that "when the tyres get cold, it's pretty nasty", adding that a cold, wet Vegas would be "horrible" without a better tyre. Brazil runner-up Max Verstappen said the working range is too small and that cold tyres are "really like a rock". Williams driver Carlos Sainz also said the wet and intermediate tyres need to improve.

## What to watch

If FP1 is wet, expect teams to run long laps to find tyre temperature rather than chase lap times. A dry Saturday night race would make the issue a 2027 development question for Pirelli rather than a factor in Vegas.
