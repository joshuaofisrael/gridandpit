Mercedes driver George Russell has launched the GR63 Karting Academy, a program at Kart Silverstone in England meant to find young drivers whose families cannot afford the usual route through karting, [Silverstone announced](https://www.silverstone.co.uk/news/george-russell-launches-gr63-karting-academy-silverstone-find-stars-tomorrow) on Thursday.

The academy is open to boys and girls aged 8 and 9. Costs are kept low for the first two stages, and everything is fully funded from the championship stage onward, according to [Silverstone's announcement](https://www.silverstone.co.uk/news/george-russell-launches-gr63-karting-academy-silverstone-find-stars-tomorrow).

## How the four stages work

Silverstone lists four steps:

- **Arrive and Drive Qualifiers, 6 to 31 December 2026.** Open sessions at Silverstone Kart Track priced at £25. The best 80 drivers move on.
- **GR63 Shootout, 31 January 2027.** The 80 qualifiers race for 25 places. [Autosport reported](https://www.autosport.com/f1/news/russell-and-brdc-launch-karting-academy/10874110/) the entry fee for this stage is £75.
- **GR63 Championship, March to November 2027.** Eight rounds at Kart Silverstone, each on a different track layout, fully funded for all 25 drivers, who also receive academy kit.
- **Top 5 Weekend Shootout, December 2027.** The top five are assessed on talent, lap times, fitness, race craft and media skills. The winner gets a fully funded karting season in 2028.

Autosport added that all drivers must be citizens of a Commonwealth country, so the program is not open to American families.

## Why Russell is doing it

"Karting is where it all started for me," Russell said in Silverstone's release, adding that he knows how hard it is for talented young drivers to get a first chance when budgets are tight. [Formula1.com reported](F1_GR) that he had teased the project earlier in the week.

The British Racing Drivers' Club, which owns Silverstone, is backing the academy. BRDC president David Coulthard said the club is committed to reinvesting in the sport and developing home-grown talent, per [Silverstone](https://www.silverstone.co.uk/news/george-russell-launches-gr63-karting-academy-silverstone-find-stars-tomorrow).

**Context:** Russell's announcement comes days after Mercedes clinched the 2026 constructors' championship in Sao Paulo, which we covered in our [race report](/articles/2026-11-09-mercedes-clinches-constructors-title-sao-paulo/). A fully funded championship with a fixed age window gives families a lower-cost way into karting than paying for a full season themselves.
