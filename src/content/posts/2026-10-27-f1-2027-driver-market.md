The biggest confirmed move for 2027 so far is at the American team Haas, which has signed Formula 2 championship leader Rafael Câmara on a multiyear contract, the [team announced](https://www.haasf1team.com/news/rafael-camara-confirmed-tgr-haas-f1-team-2027) on October 15. He replaces Esteban Ocon, who leaves at the end of 2026 and has no seat for next year, [AP reported](https://apnews.com/article/rafael-camara-haas-f1-f2fbef04937fa066638141ccba6e6536).

## Haas: Câmara in, Bearman expected to stay

Câmara is 21, a member of the Ferrari driver academy, and the reigning Formula 3 champion, according to [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/ferrari-junior-rafael-camara-promoted-to-f1-with-haas-for-2027.1RSxvnFaLu8Hd3JD2ppFsz). Haas says he tested its older car at Portimão in August. [BBC Sport reported](https://www.bbc.com/sport/formula1/articles/cr15jn74l0pdo) that he was picked over Leonardo Fornaroli, Jack Doohan and Ryo Hirakawa.

Haas has not yet confirmed its other driver. Team principal Ayao Komatsu told the BBC: "It is our intention to continue with Ollie. It is just a matter of timing and finalising certain details."

## Cadillac: no confirmation

Cadillac has raced 2026 with Sergio Pérez and Valtteri Bottas. In Austin, team principal Marcin Budkowski declined to say whether either driver is under contract for 2027, saying the team would make "official releases" when the time is right, [Motorsport Week reported](https://www.motorsportweek.com/2026/10/24/cadillac-f1-drivers-in-contact/). He said "pretty much all" drivers on the market had contacted him, without naming anyone.

## What to watch

* An official Haas announcement on Oliver Bearman.
* Any Cadillac statement on Pérez, Bottas or test driver Colton Herta.
* Where Esteban Ocon lands, if anywhere.
