Rafael Camara will race for Haas again at this weekend's Las Vegas Grand Prix. Formula 1 [posted on X](XF1) on Monday morning that the Brazilian "will return for his second F1 race weekend", and that Ryo Hirakawa "is also set to get behind the wheel during FP1".

## Why Haas have a seat to fill

Haas have had a vacant race seat for the last five rounds since Esteban Ocon and team principal Ayao Komatsu fell out in Mexico last month, [Sky Sports reports](https://www.skysports.com/f1/news/13612731/rafael-camara-haas-keep-brazilian-driver-as-oliver-bearmans-team-mate-for-las-vegas-gp-following-home-debut). Hirakawa raced in Mexico City, then Camara, already confirmed as a Haas driver for 2027, made his debut at his home race in Sao Paulo.

[Autosport](https://www.autosport.com/f1/news/haas-sticks-with-rafael-camara-for-las-vegas-ryo-hirakawa-in-for-fp1/10874781/) says Hirakawa was unavailable for Brazil because he was racing in the World Endurance Championship finale at Monza, where he went on to win his third WEC title. He scored a point on his F1 debut in Mexico City after post-race penalties for other drivers.

## A tough first weekend

Camara's debut came in changeable, wet conditions. Autosport says he showed good pace by the end of third practice, but hit the wall in Q2 and spent a difficult stint on intermediate tyres that left him near the back. Komatsu told Sky Sports the debut, in very difficult conditions, showed why the team signed him, and that Haas want to build on it.

## FP1 and what comes next

Hirakawa will take Oliver Bearman's car in first practice in Las Vegas, his fourth practice outing of the season, according to Sky Sports. Haas have not confirmed who will drive alongside Bearman at the final two rounds in Qatar and Abu Dhabi. Both outlets note that Camara leads the Formula 2 standings, by seven points per Sky Sports, with those two rounds still to run, so he may need to choose between F1 experience and the F2 title, or try to do both.
