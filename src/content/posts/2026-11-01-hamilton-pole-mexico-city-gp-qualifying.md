Lewis Hamilton will start the Mexico City Grand Prix from pole position after a Q3 lap of 1m 16.514s, just 0.009 seconds quicker than George Russell, according to the [FIA qualifying classification](https://www.fia.com/events/fia-formula-one-world-championship/season-2026/mexico-city-grand-prix/qualifying). It is his first pole for Ferrari and his first anywhere since the 2023 Hungarian Grand Prix, [BBC Sport](https://www.bbc.com/sport/formula1/articles/c6reyl8zp3jwo) reported, and the 105th of his career.

## The top of the grid

Charles Leclerc qualified third. Max Verstappen set the fourth fastest time but drops one place for his collision with Liam Lawson in FP2, so championship leader Kimi Antonelli moves up to start fourth, the [official starting grid](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2026/races/1298/mexico/starting-grid) shows. Lando Norris, Isack Hadjar, Arvid Lindblad, Pierre Gasly and Nico Hulkenberg complete the top ten starters.

Lawson had set the eighth fastest time, but the grid notes say he was disqualified from qualifying for a technical breach and races at the stewards' discretion from the back. Valtteri Bottas also starts at the rear after taking several new power unit parts.

## What it means for the title

The front row is made up of the two drivers who can still stop Antonelli clinching the championship on Sunday. According to the BBC, that only happens if Russell wins and Antonelli finishes lower than ninth, or if Hamilton wins and Antonelli scores no points.
