F1 Academy revealed its 2027 calendar on 10 November, setting a seven-round fifth season that will continue to support Formula 1 in Asia, Europe and North America. The [announcement on Formula1.com](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/f1-academy-unveils-calendar-for-2027-season.1mYuyMEiCaWyI8pAfg5K0O), published at 3:02 p.m. UTC, lists the following dates and venues:

| Date | Venue |
| --- | --- |
| March 19-21 | Jeddah Corniche Circuit, Saudi Arabia |
| April 16-18 | Shanghai International Circuit, China |
| May 21-23 | Circuit Gilles-Villeneuve, Canada |
| July 2-4 | Silverstone Circuit, Great Britain |
| July 30 – August 1 | Hungaroring, Hungary |
| October 22-24 | Circuit of The Americas, United States |
| November 18-20 | Las Vegas Strip Circuit, United States |

The season will open in Jeddah in March and close under the lights of the Las Vegas Strip in November. Between those races, the series will visit Shanghai in April and Montreal in May, then run a European double-header at Silverstone and, for the first time, the Hungaroring. Formula 1's report said Budapest will become the 17th circuit to host an F1 Academy round. Two later trips to the United States complete the year, at the Circuit of The Americas in late October and Las Vegas in November.

Susie Wolff, F1 Academy's managing director, said: "It's hard to believe we're already heading into our fifth season of F1 Academy. What we've collectively achieved in such a short space of time is something we can all be proud of, and I'm equally excited about what lies ahead."

She added: "With our 2027 calendar, we're continuing to build consistency in where we race, which is important for our drivers, teams and fans. I'm hugely grateful to our promoters for their continued support and belief in what we're building." Wolff also said each season has brought more fans, more young girls into motorsport and a rising level of competition, and that she wants that level pushed higher in 2027.

The calendar is as published by Formula 1 and F1 Academy on 10 November 2026.
